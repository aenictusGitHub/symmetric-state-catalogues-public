CONVENTIONS, CONSTRUCTIONS & EVIDENCE

What the geometry means

A reproducible atlas of pure symmetric states, not a convexification of their images.

Sector lengths

Every state is a normalized vector in the symmetric subspace of $N$ qubits, equivalently spin $j=N/2$. Coefficients are ordered by excitation number $k=0,\ldots,N$ in the normalized Dicke basis.

$$|\psi\rangle = \sum_{k=0}^{N} c_k |D_N^{(k)}\rangle,\qquad \sum_k |c_k|^2 = 1.$$

The plotted $S_k$ is the sum of squared expectation values of all $N$-qubit Pauli strings of weight $k$; there is no division by the binomial coefficient. $S_0=1$, and $\sum_{k=1}^{N}S_k=2^N-1$. Collective rotations and global phase leave every sector length invariant. The coordinates do not uniquely specify a state or a Majorana constellation.

The five-qubit boundary

Write $x=S_1$, $y=S_2$. The lower boundary $b(x)$ consists of an algebraic $C_2$ arc from $x=0$ to $x_c=(22805-9840\sqrt5)/11881\approx0.0675104033$, the arc $(3x-2\sqrt{5x}+15)/4$ for $x_c\le x\le9/5$, and the line $2x$ for $9/5\le x\le5$. The small arc is sampled from its exact state parametrization, rather than approximated by a convex hull. The zero-dipole endpoint is $(0,18/5)$. Exact parameter definitions and state constructors are in the downloadable source files.

The complete-range assertion follows the dedicated The exact $(S_1,S_2)$ range of pure symmetric five-qubit states: A compact rigorous proof and the completed five-qubit manuscript. It supersedes the older manuscript text in which exceptional reduction branches were still open. The dataset records those sources explicitly.

The six-qubit outer region

The surface is exported from the current analytical-bound renderer. It includes the paired-purity bound, multipole and reduced-state compatibility bounds, the established small-dipole roof cuts, and the refined four-copy condition. In $x=S_1$, $y=S_2$, $z=S_3$, the last condition is

$$\begin{aligned}&\frac5{21}(y+z-5x-5)+\left(\frac{162+8x-10y-3z}{231}\right)^2\\&\qquad\ge\frac{(8+2x-z)(401+137x+35y+5z)}{51744}.\end{aligned}$$

This is $8q_2+(64/81)q_4^2\ge2q_0q_6$ in double-copy total-spin notation. Its equality states are exactly the coherent products: the new lower cap and its open creases are rigorously unattainable, except at $(6,15,20)$. This bound is necessary and is not asserted to be sharp elsewhere.

The violet patches and selectable samples come from explicitly verified $C_2$–$C_6$ constructions, their superpositions, the paired-purity face, the zero-dipole side, and established named states including $\omega$ and $E_*$. Every selectable violet point carries actual complex Dicke coefficients. No state is assigned to a gray junction. The surface mesh is a finite-resolution approximation to the analytical outer body; the underlying state samples retain their independently recomputed coordinates. The refined display first applies three conforming local refinement passes near attainment transitions, then clips mixed triangles with 20 bisection steps and projects the transition points onto the analytical graph. Shared normals smooth each sheet while keeping genuine creases distinct. The color transition and surface mesh remain finite-resolution display approximations; they do not provide a separate exact witness for every interpolated triangle point.

Selectable six-qubit boundary families

Eight added states sample the curved color seam of the filtered-octahedral patch on the lower plane. With $D_k=|D_6^{(k)}\rangle$, write $|F_t\rangle=A_tD_0+B_tD_3-C_tD_6$ for $0\le t\le1$, where

$A_t=\sqrt{\frac{4+5t+3\sqrt{t(t+8)}}{18}},\quad B_t=\frac{\sqrt{5(1-t)}}3,\quad C_t=\sqrt{\frac{4+5t-3\sqrt{t(t+8)}}{18}}.$

Its coordinates are $(S_1,S_2,S_3)=(2t(t+8)/3,5+10t^2,20t(4-t)/3)$. The samples use $t=1/9,\ldots,8/9$, and the full curve is traced in violet with lighter dashed rear portions; the endpoints are collectively equivalent to octahedral and product states. The normalized amplitudes and exact rational sector tuples are evaluated directly, and checked independently with reduced purities.

Seven further states sample the fully attained straight supporting-plane segment from the $C_5$ junction to $|\omega\rangle$. The displayed canonical radical family is defined in the selected state’s expandable parameters; the complete construction and equality classification are in the six-qubit supplement. The fully attained segment is traced in violet; it is a state locus inside the flat supporting face, rather than an additional geometric body crease.

The nearby curved rank-four outer crease is a different locus. Its open part admits no pure symmetric state. To see the distinction, the complete sharp-plane classification restricts every equality tuple to

$(x,y,z)=\left(\frac32-\frac{474q}{169},\frac{99}{10}+\frac{3234q}{845},11-\frac{2300q}{169}\right),\quad0\le q\le\frac{169}{484}.$

The stronger retained paired-spectral condition defining the drawn curved crease is strict on every point of that physical segment except its $C_5$ endpoint. Thus the open curved crease contains no pure-state sector tuple; a self-contained verification for the retained bound is included. The exceptional $|E_*\rangle$ has its own equality orbit at a point of the same straight segment. Consequently the atlas samples the actual attained segment beside the gray curve, and assigns no state to the excluded curve.

Samples on the paired-purity face border

The additional violet/gray boundary samples lie on $\Delta=15+5S_1-S_2-S_3=0$. There are six samples on its straight portion and ten on its elliptic portion. Its straight portion is $(x,y,z)=(x,5+15x,10-10x)$, $1/6\le x\le1/3$, and is parameterized by $x=(1+4r^2)/6$, $0\le r\le1/2$. The displayed state $|L_r\rangle$ is the normalized cofactor vector of an explicit real-Hodge matrix $K(r)-I$; its full matrix and chosen nonzero cofactor chart are defined in the expandable parameters. These are actual pure states, generally with no nontrivial cyclic rotation symmetry. The state construction and verification are included.

The adjoining elliptic boundary has the compact normalized state

$|P_s\rangle=\frac14\left(A_sD_1+\sqrt{10}D_3+\frac{s}{A_s}D_5\right),\quad A_s=\sqrt{3+\sqrt{9-s^2}},\quad-1\le s\le3,$

and exact coordinates $(x,y,z)=((9-s^2)/24,(45+5s)/4,(135-30s-5s^2)/24)$. The signed $D_5$ amplitude joins both elliptic branches continuously. At $s=-1$ it joins the straight border; at $s=0$ it reaches $S_1=3/8$, and at $s=3$ it reaches the GHZ sector tuple. Every added state is independently checked by reduced purities and comes with its exact sector tuple and Majorana constellation. This follows the complete paired-face theorem in the proved-region supplement.

Majorana convention

A constellation is the unordered multiset of $N$ constituent qubit spinors whose symmetrized tensor product gives the state. In this atlas, $|0\rangle$ is the north pole and $|1\rangle$ the south pole. Roots $\zeta=b/a$ of

$$M(\zeta)=\sum_{k=0}^{N}(-1)^k\sqrt{\binom Nk}\,c_k\,\zeta^{N-k}$$

give the spinor directions $a|0\rangle+b|1\rangle$, with missing highest-degree roots at infinity. The Bloch coordinates are $(2\operatorname{Re}\zeta,2\operatorname{Im}\zeta,1-|\zeta|^2)/(1+|\zeta|^2)$. Coincident stars are shown once with their multiplicity; front and rear stars have different brightness. The optional hull connects constellation points on the sphere. It is unrelated to taking a convex hull in sector-length space.

Stars are computed locally from the displayed coefficients, including repeated poles and roots at infinity. The state is reconstructed from the computed stars, and the fidelity is shown under “Dicke coefficients & verification.” The displayed orientation is a representative: dragging the sphere changes the view, not the stored coefficients. Hover previews a point; clicking pins the corresponding state until another selection.

Symmetry axes, navigation and export

The $C_n$ axes are proper rotations preserving the complete constellation and its star multiplicities. The order selector can show the highest order, one chosen order, or all detected axes; the rear halves are dashed. Rear curve dashes retain a consistent on-screen size when zooming, and line layers leave face-depth visibility unchanged. For continuous axial symmetry the principal $C_\infty$ axis is shown. Balanced Dicke constellations additionally have a continuous family of transverse $C_2$ axes, recorded in the summary without replacing that continuum by arbitrary discrete axes. Reflections are not counted as rotations. “Align highest $C_n$ with $z$” rotates the displayed constellation so a highest-order axis coincides with the vertical $z$ direction. The stored Dicke coefficients retain their original basis; the image is a collectively rotated view of that representative. Reset restores the original frame, and SVG export includes the chosen display alignment.

Drag the $N=3,4,5$ sets to move them. In the three-dimensional $N=6$ view, use Shift-drag or right-drag to move, and ordinary drag to rotate. Sector plots support up to $80\times$ magnification centered on the pointer. Reset view restores the initial framing. The Export SVG buttons preserve the displayed camera, pan and zoom, geometry, symmetry axes, point markers, labels and leaders. Fonts are embedded; mathematical labels use XHTML inside SVG foreignObject elements. No raster screenshot is embedded. The constellation hull draws polygon boundaries, with its fill triangulation kept invisible.

Evidence and reproducibility

Named states and sampled families are stored in ordinary JSON and JavaScript data files. The browser recomputes sector lengths from symmetric reduced density matrices. The source package includes deterministic generators, independent audits, the exact $N=3$ and $N=4$ hover constructors, the $N=5$ boundary constructors, and the provenance of the $N=6$ export. Failed numerical fitting is never treated as exclusion.
