SELECTABLE N6 BOUNDARY SAMPLES: ATTAINMENT AND THE EXCLUDED CURVED CREASE

Conventions
-----------
D_k = |D_6^(k)> is the normalized symmetric Dicke vector, k excitations.
x=S1, y=S2, z=S3; Theta=sum_{k=0}^6 (-1)^k c_k c_{6-k}.
For a pure symmetric six-qubit state:
x=(2/3)|<J>|^2,

1. Filtered-octahedral C3 seam: eight actual states
------------------------------------------------
For 0<=t<=1 define
 A=(4+5t+3 sqrt(t(t+8)))/18,
 B=5(1-t)/9,
 C=(4+5t-3 sqrt(t(t+8)))/18,
 |F_t> = sqrt(A) D0 + sqrt(B) D3 - sqrt(C) D6.
A,B,C are nonnegative and sum to1. Furthermore AC=4(1-t)^2/81,
Theta=-2sqrt(AC)-B=-(1-t), <Jz>=3(A-C)=sqrt(t(t+8)),
and <Jx>=<Jy>=0. The quadrupole is axial, with eigenvalues
(-5t/2,-5t/2,5t), hence ||Q||^2=75t^2/2.
Therefore
 (x,y,z)=(2t(t+8)/3, 5+10t^2, 20t(4-t)/3).
In particular z=5x+5-y, the p2=0 supporting plane. This is the exact
C3 boundary of the already proved filtered-octa patch. It is a color seam
inside a flat body face, not a new geometric ridge.
The new sample parameters are t=j/9, j=1,...,8.
Source: Julia/N6_filtered_octa_attainment.jl, n6fo_c3_state/point.

2. The attained C5-to-omega straight segment: seven actual states
---------------------------------------------------------------
The explicit seven-Dicke-level radical family R_t is provided in the
sample metadata, including every auxiliary variable. Its source is
Julia/N6_rankfour_equality_segment.jl, n6rank4_equality_state.
For 1<=t<=sqrt(7/5),
 q=|Theta|^2=[13(t^2-1)/(4(5-2t^2))]^2,
 (x,y,z)=(3/2-474q/169, 99/10+3234q/845, 11-2300q/169),
 0<=q<=169/484.
Every point satisfies 65y+63z-217x=1011. The endpoints are the C5
junction and omega. The complete equality-state theorem proves that
this straight segment is the FULL attainable sector image on that
supporting plane (including the exceptional C2 orbit at an interior
sector point). The new samples use t=(40+j)/40, j=1,...,7.
These are actual normalized state vectors, not superpositions inferred
from line interpolation. All six sectors are independently recomputed
from Dicke marginal purities in build_n6_boundary_samples.py.

3. Why no violet states can be put on the annotated curved crease
----------------------------------------------------------------
The first annotated curve is the rank-four-plane / retained paired-
spectral crease. Its equation is retained paired margin=0, in addition
to the supporting-plane equality. The existing complete equality theorem
restricts any attaining pure state to the straight segment in section2.
Here is a direct exact check that its open portion misses that crease.

Put Delta=15+5x-y-z, v=sqrt(Delta/40). Along the segment,
 3/55<=v<=1/5,
 q=169(1-25v^2)/448,
 g=37+3x-5y+48q+sqrt(40Delta)
  =(2/7)(1-5v)(135v-1).
The retained paired bound is g>=max(R1,R2), where
 a=1/4+v/2, h=1/4+v+5v^2,
 k=1+(2sqrt(2)-8)a+(22-4sqrt(2))a^2,
 R1=4Delta q/k,
 R2=16Delta q/[sqrt(h+4a sqrt(q))+sqrt(h)]^2.
On this interval
 k-h=(2v+1)[1+2sqrt(2)+(2-4sqrt(2))v]/8>0,
so R1<=4Delta q/h. The squared denominator of R2 is >=4h,
so R2<=4Delta q/h as well. The exact factorization
 g h-4Delta q=(5v-1)^2(305v^2+126v-1)/14
is strictly positive for 3/55<=v<1/5. Indeed the final polynomial
is increasing there and has value4102/605>0 at3/55. Consequently
 g>max(R1,R2)
for every segment point except the C5 junction, where v=1/5,q=0.
Thus the curved crease itself has no attaining states except that
junction. Omega lies inside the plane face, away from the curved crease.
The new selectable R_t points show the actual nearby attainable locus,
without falsely certifying the gray outer crease.
