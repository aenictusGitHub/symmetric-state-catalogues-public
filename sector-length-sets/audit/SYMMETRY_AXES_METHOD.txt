Proper rotational axes of the Majorana multiset
==============================================

Directions returned by symmetry-axes.js use the original Bloch coordinates.
The current viewer maps them to its world frame by (x,y,z) -> (x,z,-y).
This is a proper orthogonal change of frame and preserves the axis orders.

For any non-axial constellation choose two non-collinear star directions a,b.
A proper rotation is uniquely determined by the ordered images a',b'. It must
preserve their dot product and their multiplicities. For every admissible image
pair, construct the unique orientation-preserving rotation using orthonormal
frames. Keep it only if every star maps to a star of the SAME multiplicity.
This enumerates the complete finite rotational group, up to the stated numeric
tolerance. It is stronger than guessing axes from individual star directions or
face normals, which can miss valid twofold axes in generic orientations.

For each nonidentity rotation recover its unoriented axis and finite order.
The order is the least k<=N for which k times its angle is a multiple of 2pi.
This handles rotations of angle 4pi/5 correctly. A non-axial N-star multiset has
an off-axis orbit of the full rotational order, so that order cannot exceed N.
Merge collinear unoriented axes and retain only the maximal order on each axis.
Thus an octahedron has 3 C4, 4 C3, and 6 C2 axes; its C4 axes are not listed a
second time as C2 axes. Reflections are excluded from this proper-rotation list.

One occupied direction and two antipodal occupied directions have continuous
rotations about their principal axis, denoted C_infinity. If the antipodal
multiplicities agree, there is also a continuous family of transverse pi
rotations. The interface should describe this family rather than draw an
arbitrary finite subset as if it were the full set of C2 axes.

Axis detection is numerical geometry of the displayed representative, not a
new equality classification or a claim about every state at its sector tuple.
The default positional tolerance is 2e-6. The result includes the tolerance,
verified maximum permutation error, and a reliability flag. Nearly collinear
unresolved configurations are reported without inventing an axis catalogue.
