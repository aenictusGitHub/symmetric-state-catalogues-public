A global sum-of-squares certificate improves the rigorous lower bound. Optimality of the known algebraic representative remains unproved.

The rigorous interval

The minimization is over normalized pure symmetric twelve-qubit states. The upper endpoint is exactly attained by the algebraic representative. The rational lower endpoint follows from the certificate below; equality is not claimed.

Proof by a sum-of-squares identity

The polynomial vectors have degree 4. The rational Gram matrices are symmetric and positive definite. For a nonzero state, division by the positive power of q gives the bound. The verifier independently derives the target from reduced Dicke purities, checks every complex polynomial coefficient and proves matrix positivity by exact leading principal minors.

The check covers 110982 monomials including cancellations, 73 positive matrices and 16016 weighted absolute squares. No numerical tolerance enters the proof.
