Why this is an exact global proof

  1. Complete invariant space. A character count for physical rotations and Kramers mixing gives dimension 1794−1504=290. Direct raising/lowering checks verify the covariants and all transvectant intertwiners.
  2. Exact zeros. Every certificate vector vanishes on the full Kramers plane of the analytical witness. The target value and its three independent parameter derivatives vanish exactly in the degree-eleven number field.
  3. Exact matrix definition. Each Gram matrix is the identity plus symmetric corrections at 286 specified positions. Those corrections are defined by 286 exact polynomial-evaluation equations. A rigorous interval solve certifies the unique solution.
  4. Full polynomial identity. The 286 evaluation functionals and four exact vanishing functionals are injective on the complete 290-dimensional invariant space. A rigorously nonzero determinant proves this. Agreement on these functionals therefore proves equality for every complex state.
  5. Strict positivity. Certified Gershgorin bounds prove that every Gram matrix is at least 99/100 times the identity.

The earlier rank deficit arose from checking the unaveraged expression in a larger space. Kramers averaging supplies the missing structural reduction; the proof does not infer an exact identity from a small numerical residual.
