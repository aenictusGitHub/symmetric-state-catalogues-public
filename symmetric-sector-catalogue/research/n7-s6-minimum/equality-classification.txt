Complete equality classification for pure symmetric seven-qubit states
====================================================================

Assume the independently verified positive-definite sum-of-squares identity
for q(P6-7q^2), with q=<psi|psi>. The following argument proves:

S6(psi)=7 if and only if psi is a collective SU(2) rotation of
 alpha |D7^0> + beta |D7^7>, |alpha|^2+|beta|^2=1.

This includes coherent states when alpha or beta vanishes, and the entire
complex generalized-GHZ family, not just balanced GHZ states.

1. Equality forces a rank-two Hankel condition.
Write c_r=sqrt(C(7,r))*x_r and associate the binary septic
 f(X,Y)=sum_(r=0)^7 C(7,r) x_r X^(7-r)Y^r.
Every positive summand in the exact SOS must vanish. The holomorphic cubic
blocks have twice-spins 3,5,9 and therefore 4+6+10=20 scalar components.
Exact rational linear algebra shows that their span is precisely the span
of the twenty 3x3 minors of
 H=(x_(i+j))_(0<=i<=2,0<=j<=5).
Consequently rank(H)<=2. The supplied verification script checks the full
20x120 coefficient matrices, an invertible20x20 change of basis, and the
positive Gram matrices of all three blocks.

2. An elementary apolar argument gives only two possible forms.
A nonzero left-kernel vector (u0,u1,u2) of H gives
 (u0 d_X^2+u1 d_X d_Y+u2 d_Y^2)f=0,
because the coefficient of X^(5-j)Y^j is exactly
 42 C(5,j)(u0 x_j+u1 x_(j+1)+u2 x_(j+2)), j=0,...,5.
Over the complex numbers this nonzero quadratic differential operator
factors into two linear operators.
If they are distinct, an invertible coordinate change turns it into
 d_U d_V, so homogeneity implies f=A U^7+B V^7.
If they coincide, the operator becomes d_V^2, so
 f=A U^7+B U^6 V.
This argument includes every rank-one/rank-two boundary case. In the
two-power case A=0 or B=0 gives a coherent power. In the tangent case
B=0 is coherent; A=0 can remain noncoherent and is covered by Step4.

3. The mixed cubic forces orthogonality in the two-power case.
Because equality is preserved by collective rotations, rotate the first
linear form to X. In the noncoherent two-power case the state is then
 f=A X^7+B(cX+dY)^7,
with A,B nonzero and d nonzero (the two linear forms are distinct).
The highest component of the mixed(2,1) block with twice-spin13 is
 h=(x0*x2-x1^2)*conj(x5)
   +(x0*x3-x1*x2)*conj(x6)
   +(x1*x3-x2^2)*conj(x7).
Substituting x_r=A*delta_(r,0)+B*c^(7-r)*d^r gives exactly
 h=A*|B|^2*c^4*conj(c)*d^2*conj(d)^5*(|c|^2+|d|^2).
The Gram coefficient of this block is strictly positive. Equality therefore
requires h=0. With A,B,d nonzero and |c|^2+|d|^2>0, this forces c=0.
Thus the two coherent spinors are orthogonal, and f is A X^7+B'Y^7.

4. Noncoherent tangent forms are impossible.
Rotate the repeated linear form U to X. Absorbing any X component of V
into the X^7 term leaves support only at x0 and x1, with x1 nonzero for a
noncoherent tangent. Component4 (zero-based magnetic-component index) of
the same twice-spin13 mixed covariant restricts to
 -5*x1^2*conj(x1).
It cannot vanish when x1 is nonzero. Hence every tangent equality state
is coherent and again belongs to the generalized-GHZ family.

5. Sufficiency.
For arbitrary complex alpha,beta with |alpha|^2+|beta|^2=1, direct Pauli
expectations, or the exact reduced-purity checker, give
 (S0,S2,S4,S6)=(1,21,35,7)
on alpha D7^0+beta D7^7. Collective rotations preserve all sector lengths.
This proves the converse and completes the classification.

Reproducibility:
 verify_equality_classification.py checks the Hankel-minor span,
 mixed-cubic formulas, their factorization/restriction and positivity.
 verify_even_sectors.py independently checks the full complex endpoint
 family and P2+P4+P6=63q^2 from reduced purities.
The global SOS identity is independently verified by the separate
certificate verifier; this equality argument depends on that identity.
