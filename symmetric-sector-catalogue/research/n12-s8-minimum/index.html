Why the finite certificate is a global proof

The standalone verifier independently checks the ingredients needed for the entire polynomial identity.

  1. Complete invariant space. Exact raising- and lowering-operator checks establish covariance. Weight counts and exact highest-weight ranks prove that the real, conjugation-symmetric invariant polynomials of bidegree (4,4) form a 250-dimensional space.
  2. Exact vanishing. Every kernel vector vanishes at the analytical state. The target has zero value and zero derivatives in the three independent state parameters, with the bound held fixed. These identities are checked exactly modulo the defining polynomial.
  3. Exact coefficient equations. The certificate fixes the free Gram entries as rational numbers and defines the remaining 246 entries by 246 coefficient equations. A rigorous interval matrix solve proves existence and uniqueness.
  4. Full identity. The 246 coefficient functionals, together with the four vanishing functionals, give an injective map on the complete 250-dimensional invariant space. Certified nonsingularity proves that agreement on these functionals is equality of the full polynomials, for every complex state.
  5. Positivity. Directed-rounding real-ball arithmetic encloses the exact Gram entries and their LDL pivots. Every pivot is strictly positive, so no numerical tolerance assumption enters the inequality.
