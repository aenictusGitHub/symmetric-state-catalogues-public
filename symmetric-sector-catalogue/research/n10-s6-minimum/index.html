Why the finite certificate proves the full identity

The coefficients of the matrices are defined by an exact finite linear system, avoiding enormous expanded algebraic fractions. The independent verifier establishes the full identity as follows.

  1. Complete invariant space. The target and every Gram polynomial are invariant under collective SU(2), have bidegree (4,4) in the amplitudes and their conjugates, and have real coefficients symmetric under conjugation. This space has dimension 130. Exact raising- and lowering-operator checks establish covariance; exact highest-weight ranks verify a complete holomorphic basis.
  2. Four exact vanishing conditions. On the real ansatz with amplitudes (1,√u,t,z√u) at Dicke indices (0,3,6,9), the target has zero value and zero derivatives with respect to u, t and z. These are checked by exact reduction modulo the degree-14 polynomial, holding s⋆ constant during differentiation. Every squared kernel vector also has zero value and first derivatives.
  3. 126 coefficient equations. The certificate specifies 126 monomial coefficients and 126 unknown Gram entries. All remaining entries are exact rational constants. The selected entries are defined as the unique solution making those polynomial coefficients agree. A rigorous ball-matrix solve proves that this system is nonsingular and encloses its exact solution.
  4. Full identity, not interpolation. The 126 coefficient functionals together with the four vanishing functionals form an injective map on the complete 130-dimensional invariant space. The verifier proves nonsingularity of this augmented matrix. Thus agreement on these 130 functionals proves equality of the entire polynomials for all complex amplitudes.
  5. Certified positivity. Directed-rounding real-ball arithmetic encloses every exact Gram entry and every LDL pivot. All pivots are strictly greater than the rational number 1/25. This proves positivity without a numerical tolerance assumption.

Reproduce and audit the proof

Install python-flint (the tested dependency is pinned), then run from this proof directory:

python3 verify_certificate.py

The verifier uses exact rational/algebraic arithmetic and certified Arb balls at 768-bit precision. It does not use an optimizer, NumPy or SciPy. Numerical optimization was used only to discover the certificate.

The linear solve uses the rigorous precond algorithm; the approximate, unbounded-error mode is not used. See the python-flint matrix documentation and FLINT’s verified matrix algorithms.
