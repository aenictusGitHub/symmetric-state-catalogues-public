Relation to spin-three condensates

The paired threefold and fivefold families occur in the spin-three Bose–Einstein-condensate literature, where rotationally invariant quartic energies share the same degeneracy. See Barnett, Turner and Demler (2007) and Kawaguchi and Ueda (2011). Their phase classification motivates the equality states. The universal fourth-sector lower bound here is established independently by the exact polynomial certificate.
