Proof of the upper bound

The polynomial vectors have degree four; all rational Gram matrices are symmetric and positive definite. Division by q to the fourth power proves the upper bound for every nonzero complex state. The independent verifier derives the sector polynomial from reduced density matrices, expands the complete identity and checks positive leading principal minors exactly.

The verification covers 110982 monomials including cancellations and 73 positive matrices. Numerical optimization discovered the certificate, but no numerical tolerance enters the proof.
