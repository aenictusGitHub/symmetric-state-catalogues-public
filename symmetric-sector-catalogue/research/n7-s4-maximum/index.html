Polynomial data conventions

In basis.json, variable indices 0–7 stand for the eight binomial-scaled amplitudes, and indices 8–15 for their conjugates. Each monomial is a sorted list of three indices and a rational coefficient. Divide each channel by its corresponding basisScales value in the certificate before applying its matrix. Component weights are reciprocal binomial coefficients. These explicit finite data completely specify the identity; the proof does not rely on the numerical discovery procedure.

The optional basis generator constructs binary-form transvectants and restricts them to the exact attaining family. It uses SymPy. The standalone verifier needs only Python’s standard library.
