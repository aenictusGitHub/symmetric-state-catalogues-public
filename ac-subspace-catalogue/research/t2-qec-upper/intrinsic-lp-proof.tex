\title{Exact feasible enumerators for the second-order AC relaxation}
\author{}\date{}\maketitle
\paragraph{Result and scope.}
The supplied rational points satisfy the expanded necessary-condition
relaxation at $(N,k)=(35,8),(41,9),(42,9)$. Thus this particular relaxation
cannot lower the existing upper endpoints at those sizes. They are not
physical projectors and do not prove existence of the corresponding
AC subspaces. No stronger unrestricted upper bound is obtained here.

Let $d=N+1$, $j=N/2$, and let $P$ be a hypothetical rank-$k$ AC2 projector.
Choose Hilbert--Schmidt orthonormal spin tensors $T_{\ell m}$ and define
\[
 a_\ell=\sum_m|\operatorname{Tr}(PT_{\ell m})|^2,
 \qquad q_\ell=a_\ell/k.
\]
Then
\[
 q_\ell\ge0,\quad\sum_{\ell=0}^Nq_\ell=1,\quad
 q_0=\frac{k}{d},\quad q_1=q_2=0.
\]
The intrinsic MacWilliams matrix has exact entries
\[
 M_{b a}=\frac{2b+1}{N+1}\,
 {}_4F_3\!\left(\begin{matrix}-b,b+1,-a,a+1\\1,-N,N+2\end{matrix};1\right)
 =(2b+1)(-1)^{N+a+b}
 \begin{Bmatrix}j&j&b\\j&j&a\end{Bmatrix}.
\]
The hypergeometric series terminates at $\min(a,b)$.
The physical transition enumerator is
\[
 (Mq)_b=\frac1k\sum_m\operatorname{Tr}
   (PT_{bm}PT_{bm}^\dagger).
\]
Consequently
\[
 (Mq)_1=(Mq)_2=0,\qquad Mq\ge0,\qquad q_b\le k(Mq)_b.
\]
These are the AC2 specialization of the intrinsic quantum-code
MacWilliams and Knill--Laflamme inequalities. The normalization above
corresponds to $\widetilde A=(d/k)q$ in the coding-theoretic convention
$\widetilde B=kM\widetilde A$.
Primary references are
\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22439v1}{Kubischta--Teixeira, Section IX}
and \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15372}{Teixeira's Racah-polynomial formulation}.

\paragraph{Two-copy and antiunitary refinements.}
Let $\Pi_b$ project the product of two spin-$j$ spaces onto total spin $b$.
Writing $D_{\ell\ell}=(-1)^\ell$, recoupling gives
\[
 (MDq)_b=\frac1k\operatorname{Tr}[\Pi_b(P\otimes P)]\ge0.
\]
Besides the projector bound, the swap parity yields
\[
 (MDq)_b\le\frac1k\min\!\left\{2b+1,
 \frac{k[k+(-1)^{N-b}]}2\right\}.
\]
Indeed the rank of $P\otimes P$ in the symmetric/antisymmetric code sector
is $k(k+1)/2$ or $k(k-1)/2$, respectively.
For odd $N$, spin time reversal $\Theta$ squares to $-I$.
If $k$ is also odd, the matrix of overlaps between a code frame and its
time reverse is skew-symmetric, has rank at most $k-1$, and has all singular
values at most one. Therefore
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}[P\Theta(P)]\le k-1,
 \qquad \sum_\ell(-1)^\ell q_\ell\le\frac{k-1}{k}.
\]
Here $\Theta(P)$ denotes antiunitary conjugation of the projector. This
extra inequality is imposed in the $(41,9)$ certificate.

\paragraph{Error-image and remainder projectors.}
Put $c=j(j+1)/3$. The AC2 equations imply that
\[
 E=\frac1c\sum_aJ_aPJ_a,\qquad R=I-P-E
\]
are orthogonal projectors of ranks $3k$ and $r=d-4k$.
Together with $P$ they are pairwise orthogonal. Their multipoles are scalar
multiples of those of $P$. For positive tensor rank,
\[
 f_P(\ell)=1,\quad f_E(\ell)=3-\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2c},\quad
 f_R(\ell)=-4+\frac{\ell(\ell+1)}{2c}.
\]
At rank zero use $f_P(0)=1$, $f_E(0)=3$, $f_R(0)=r/k$.
For every unordered pair $A,B\in\{P,E,R\}$, define
\[
 w_\ell^{AB}=f_A(\ell)f_B(\ell)q_\ell.
\]
Positivity of transitions between two positive projectors and positivity
of their two-copy product give
\[
 Mw^{AB}\ge0,\qquad 0\le MDw^{AB}\le
 \bigl((2b+1)/k\bigr)_{b=0}^N.
\]
For $A=B$, Cauchy--Schwarz additionally gives
\[
 w_b^{AA}\le\operatorname{rank}(A)(Mw^{AA})_b.
\]
These retain all tensor ranks, not merely ranks one and two.
The relevant purity and mutual-orthogonality equalities also follow from
the displayed moment equations; the independent verifier checks them.

\paragraph{Exact certificates.}
The files \texttt{lp-feasible-N35-k8.json},
\texttt{lp-feasible-N41-k9.json}, and
\texttt{lp-feasible-N42-k9.json} give every $q_\ell$ as a rational number.
The verifier \texttt{verify\_lp\_feasible.py} uses only the Python standard
library. It reconstructs $M$ through the independent factorial six-j sum,
then checks every equality and inequality using rational arithmetic.
The constructor instead uses the terminating hypergeometric recurrence.
No floating-point tolerance is used in the final verification.

A separate numerical scan tests the same type of relaxation at selected
larger sizes, including $N=500$. Its feasible floating-point points are
reported only as exploratory evidence. They are not an all-$N$ theorem,
physical constructions, or exact feasibility certificates.
