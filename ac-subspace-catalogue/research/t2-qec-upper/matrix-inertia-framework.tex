\section*{General matrix-valued joint-compression witnesses}
Let $A_1,\ldots,A_8$ be a Hermitian basis of the rank-one and rank-two
traceless spin tensors on $V_{N/2}$. For any auxiliary dimension $a$,
and any Hermitian $a\times a$ matrices $H_1,\ldots,H_8$, put
\[
 W=\sum_{\mu=1}^8 A_\mu\otimes H_\mu.
\]
For every second-order anticoherent projection $P$ of rank $k$,
\[
 (P\otimes I_a)W(P\otimes I_a)=0.
\]
The inertia bound therefore gives
\[
 ak\le n_0(W)+\min\{n_+(W),n_-(W)\}.
\]
This family allows arbitrary ancillary matrix coefficients and need not
be rotationally invariant. The existing scalar-polynomial auxiliary-spin
witnesses form a special case. A nonsingular witness with fewer than
$ak_0$ positive eigenvalues would exclude a candidate dimension $k_0$.
A rigorous numerical discovery must be followed by exact or interval
certification of its inertia. A zero eigenvalue contributes to the bound;
approaching a singular limiting witness does not establish an exclusion.

The accompanying search uses alternating projection between the linear
space of such witnesses and a prescribed inertia cone, with Frobenius
normalization. It is a nonconvex numerical search, so its failure gives
no mathematical impossibility result. Tests at $N=35$ with auxiliary
dimensions five and six did not produce a witness excluding dimension
eight. No improvement to an unrestricted bound is asserted.
