\begin{theorem}
No pure symmetric state of nineteen qubits is anticoherent of order five.
Consequently $K(19,t)=0$ for every $t\geq5$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $V=V_{19/2}$ for the spin-$19/2$ representation. Suppose that
$\lvert\psi\rangle\in V$ were normalized and fifth-order anticoherent.
The collective-rotation average
\[
 X=\int_{SU(2)} \bigl(U_g\lvert\psi\rangle\langle\psi\rvert U_g^\dagger\bigr)^{\otimes4}\,dg
\]
is a positive semidefinite, trace-one invariant operator on
$W=\operatorname{Sym}^4(V)$, of dimension $\binom{23}{4}=8855$.
For every irreducible spin tensor of rank $1\leq\ell\leq5$,
\[
 \Tr_1\bigl[(T_{\ell0}\otimes I^{\otimes3})X\bigr]=0. \tag{1}
\]
Indeed, the first tensor factor contracts to the vanishing tensor expectation
of each rotated state.

Here are the exact conventions used to certify that these conditions are
inconsistent with positivity. Put $b_i=\sqrt{\binom{19}{i}}\lvert D_{19}^i\rangle$,
$i=0,\ldots,19$. For a sorted four-letter word $w$, let $e_w$ be its averaged
symmetric tensor product and let $o_i(w)$ denote its occupancy. Its squared norm is
\[
 \langle e_w,e_w\rangle=
 \frac{\prod_{i=0}^{19}\binom{19}{i}^{o_i(w)}o_i(w)!}{4!}.
\]
These words are orthogonal. Raising and lowering have integer coefficients
$o_i(i+1)$ and $o_i(20-i)$, respectively. The certificate supplies exact
mutually orthogonal highest-weight columns $H_J$, their multiplicities $m_J$,
and $d_J=\operatorname{lcm}_{0\leq s\leq2J}\binom{2J}{s}$.
With $U_{J,s}=J_-^sH_J/s!$, every invariant Hermitian operator has the form
\[
 X=\sum_J\sum_{s=0}^{2J}\frac{d_J}{\binom{2J}{s}}
       U_{J,s}B_JU_{J,s}^{\dagger}.
\]
Positivity is equivalent to $B_J\succeq0$ for all $J$.
There are 37 nonzero spin blocks and $\sum_Jm_J=277$.
All equations have real coefficients, so taking the real part of each
Hermitian $B_J$ preserves positive semidefiniteness, trace, and (1).
Thus the argument covers arbitrary complex states while using real
symmetric matrices and $\sum_Jm_J(m_J+1)/2=1522$ scalar variables.

The diagonal entries of $T_{\ell0}$ are the integer-normalized discrete
orthogonal polynomials of degree $\ell$ in $2i-19$, for the uniform measure
on $i=0,\ldots,19$. Each equation $R_a(B)=0$ used below is a specified
matrix entry of (1), multiplied by 16 and divided by the greatest common
divisor of its integer coefficients. The downloadable certificate lists
1380 such valid equations. It also provides positive dyadic diagonal
matrices $S_J$, rational weights $y_a$, and rational symmetric matrices $C_J$.
Define
\[
 x_J=d_J(2J+1)S_JB_JS_J\succeq0.
\]
The following identity is verified coefficient by coefficient, using
exact integer and rational arithmetic:
\[
 \boxed{-\Tr X=\sum_{a=1}^{1380}y_aR_a(B)
                  +\sum_J\Tr(C_Jx_J).} \tag{2}
\]
Every $C_J$ is strictly positive definite: all 277 rational LDL pivots are
positive. Substitution of (1) and $\Tr X=1$ into (2) would therefore give
$-1\geq0$, a contradiction. No numerical tolerance, optimizer status,
partial-transpose condition, or assumption that the state is real enters
this conclusion.
\end{proof}
