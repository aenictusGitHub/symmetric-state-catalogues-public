Can the cyclic prescription attain the bound indefinitely?

A subspace invariant under a rotation through 2π/k cannot attain dimension k = floor(N/5) + 1 from N = 420 onward. An exact sector argument and projector inequality establish this for any choice of rotation axis.

Scope of the result: this excludes every Cₖ-invariant subspace at the stated target dimension, about any axis. Arbitrary subspaces without that symmetry remain unexcluded. No infinite family of general subspaces attaining the bound has been established here.

Numerical exploration is separate from the proof

Before deriving the obstruction, searches tested cyclic and reflection-symmetric extensions of the exact N = 24 example. Random starts and interpolation of that basis did not produce an exact candidate at the following larger target dimensions.
