Optimal analytical first-order subspaces

A family of elementary spin-basis blocks supplies the first-order maximum for every qubit number N≥4, with optimality proved by the auxiliary spin-1 bound. A separate two-dimensional construction at eleven qubits, together with an exclusion of dimension three, settles the second-order maximum there. At sixteen qubits, the exact three-dimensional second-order construction now attains the auxiliary-spin inertia upper bound and is optimal.

General optimality theorem

Every positive first-order dimension in the paper’s range of one to seventeen qubits is attained by an exact construction, including the separate small-size cases below.

First-order maximality is proved for every size. The bases below attain the auxiliary spin-1 upper bound . Read the general optimality proof ↗. The higher-order constructions retain their separately stated bounds.
