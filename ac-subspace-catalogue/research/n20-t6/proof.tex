\author{Exact three-copy certificate}

\begin{theorem}
No pure symmetric state of twenty qubits is anticoherent of order six.
Consequently
\[
 \boxed{K(20,t)=0\quad(t\ge6),\qquad t_{\max}(20)=5.}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $V=V_{10}$ and suppose that a normalized $\psi\in V$ were sixth-order
anticoherent. Its rotation-averaged three-copy moment
\[
 X=\int_{SU(2)}(U_g|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|U_g^\dagger)^{\otimes3}\,dg
\]
is positive, has trace one, and is invariant on
$W=\operatorname{Sym}^3(V)$, of dimension $\binom{23}{3}=1771$.
For every spin-tensor rank $1\le\ell\le6$,
\[
 \Tr_1[(T_{\ell0}\otimes I^{\otimes2})X]=0. \tag{1}
\]
Indeed, the first factor contracts to the zero tensor expectation of the
rotated state. Only necessary equations are being imposed here.

The accompanying certificate gives a complete physical decomposition
\[
 W=\bigoplus_J V_J\otimes\mathbb C^{m_J},\qquad
 \sum_J(2J+1)m_J=1771,
\]
with 29 nonempty spin sectors, $\sum_Jm_J=61$, and
$\sum_Jm_J(m_J+1)/2=108$ real symmetric invariant coordinates.
For clarity, use $b_i=\sqrt{\binom{20}{i}}|D_{20}^{(i)}\rangle$
and averaged symmetric words $e_{abc}$, $a\le b\le c$. With occupancies
$o_i$, their squared norms are
\[
 \|e_{abc}\|^2=\frac{\prod_i\binom{20}{i}^{o_i}o_i!}{3!}.
\]
The supplied mutually orthogonal integer highest-weight columns $H_J$
are independently checked in this metric. Their descendants
$U_{J,s}=J_-^sH_J/s!$ satisfy
\[
 U_{J,s}^\dagger U_{J,s}=\binom{2J}{s}H_J^\dagger H_J.
\]
Every real invariant operator has unique coordinates
\[
 X(B)=\sum_J d_J\sum_{s=0}^{2J}
 \frac{U_{J,s}B_JU_{J,s}^T}{\binom{2J}{s}},\qquad B_J=B_J^T,
\]
where $d_J>0$ are the certificate's denominator scales. Positivity of $X$
is equivalent to $B_J\succeq0$ for all blocks. The chosen bases, the
component-zero contractions and the trace are real. Taking real parts of
complex Hermitian multiplicity matrices preserves all these conditions
and positivity, so this parametrization loses no hypothetical complex
state.

The diagonal tensors $T_{\ell0}$ are represented by degree-$\ell$
polynomials in $2i-20$, orthogonal to all smaller degrees under uniform
summation over $i=0,\ldots,20$. The certificate selects 95 physical matrix
elements of (1); each is represented by its primitive integer coefficient
row $R_a(B)$. Independence or completeness of the selected equations is
not needed, since every candidate satisfies them.

Positive dyadic diagonal matrices $S_J$, exact rational weights $y_a$,
and exact rational symmetric matrices $C_J$ are supplied. Define
\[
 x_J=d_J(2J+1)S_JB_JS_J\succeq0.
\]
The independent verifier reconstructs every equation and the physical
trace, then verifies coefficientwise the exact identity
\[
 \boxed{-\Tr X=\sum_{a=1}^{95}y_aR_a(B)+\sum_J\Tr(C_Jx_J).} \tag{2}
\]
All 29 matrices $C_J$ are strictly positive definite, as certified by all
61 strictly positive rational LDL pivots and exact reconstruction.
For a candidate state, (1), (2) and $\Tr X=1$ give $-1\ge0$, a
contradiction. This excludes arbitrary complex sixth-order states;
higher orders are excluded by nesting. No partial-transpose assumption,
constellation symmetry or numerical infeasibility claim is used.

For attainment of order five, the supplied Julia source and AC-state
catalogue contain the normalized state
\[
 75|\psi_5\rangle=
 \sqrt{561}(|D_{20}^{(0)}\rangle+|D_{20}^{(20)}\rangle)
 +3\sqrt{209}(|D_{20}^{(5)}\rangle-|D_{20}^{(15)}\rangle)
 +\sqrt{741}|D_{20}^{(10)}\rangle.
\]
Its squared norm is $(2\cdot561+18\cdot209+741)/75^2=1$.
Reflection-symmetric probabilities cancel odd diagonal moments, and
\[
 \langle J_z^2\rangle=110/3,\qquad
 \langle J_z^4\rangle=7238/3
\]
are the normalized spin-ten traces. Support gaps eliminate shifts one
through four, and the opposite signs at indices five and fifteen cancel
the shift-five terms pairwise. Hence it is fifth-order AC and proves the
claimed maximum order five.
\end{proof}
