Why the entire span is anticoherent

Fix a one-dimensional unitary character of a finite spin-rotation group. Define

For two vectors in this same space, the character phases cancel in each matrix element. Hence, for every spin tensor ,

The averaged tensor vanishes for ranks 1 through 1, 2, 3 and 5 for the quaternion, tetrahedral, octahedral and icosahedral groups respectively. Therefore every low-rank compressed tensor vanishes, including off-diagonal basis pairs. Different characters are kept separate: their direct sum is not automatically AC.

The sign character of the octahedral group is essential at 22 qubits, where its fixed space is zero but its sign space contains a third-order AC state.

Exact verification and reproducibility

The construction checker verifies normalization, orthogonality and every ordered reduced cross matrix. A separate verifier reconstructs the polynomial ranks, rotation-character dimensions and all compressed spin moments, without importing the construction code. Each space is tested above its group-guaranteed order until an exact failure or the catalogue limit; this is a claim about this space, not a higher-order nonexistence proof.

Sources and scope

The construction uses the invariant-polynomial method of Pereira and Paul-Paddock, Anticoherent Subspaces (2017), Section 3, and the Molien series linked above. The catalogue supplies the explicit orthonormal bases and exact finite verifications for its listed qubit numbers. No claim of a new invariant-theory theorem or of global maximality is made.
