# Exact AC subspaces from binary polyhedral invariant theory

`bases.json` contains 65 orthonormal Dicke bases for the catalogue qubit numbers
1–20, 22, 24, 35, and 42. All coefficients are exact radicals. The data include
seven families: fixed subspaces of the quaternion, binary tetrahedral,
binary octahedral, and binary icosahedral groups; and one nontrivial
one-dimensional character sector for each of the first three groups.

These are constructive lower bounds. A Molien coefficient counts the specified
symmetry sector, not the maximum dimension among unrestricted AC subspaces.
Distinct character sectors must not be joined: superpositions across sectors
need not remain AC.

## Reproduction

Run `python3 construct.py` in this directory. Construction uses exact rational
arithmetic in `Q(i sqrt(3))`; SymPy is used only for formatting the normalized
coefficients and LaTeX. The output is `bases.json` and `verification.json`.
`verify_independently.py` independently verifies the resulting bases by spin
moment compression instead of reduced density matrices.

The construction checks every ordered cross reduction, including off-diagonal
terms between distinct basis vectors, successively through order 7 or the
first failure. For these records every subspace fails an order at most 6, so
`verifiedAchievedOrder` is the exact order of this particular subspace.

## Polynomial and Dicke conventions

An array `polynomialCoefficients[r]` represents the coefficient of
`x^r y^(N-r)`, including homogeneous zero endpoints. The orthogonalization
inner product is

\[
\langle p,q\rangle_N=
\sum_{r=0}^{N}\frac{\overline{p_r}q_r}{\binom Nr}.
\]

For an orthogonalized polynomial with norm squared \(h\), the normalized
Dicke vector is

\[
|u_p\rangle=\sum_{r=0}^{N}
\frac{(-1)^r p_r}{\sqrt{h\binom Nr}}|D_N^r\rangle.
\]

`polynomialCoefficientsQuadratic[r] = [a,b]` is an alternative exact encoding
of \(a+bi\sqrt3\), with rational strings. `normSquared` is the positive rational
\(h\). `sourcePolynomialBasis` names the simple monomials spanning the same
space before weighted Gram–Schmidt. Each displayed Dicke basis is genuinely
orthonormal; the source monomials generally are not.

## Generators and Hilbert series

Write

\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_4&=x^2y^2,&R_4&=x^4+y^4,&U_2&=xy,&V_4&=x^4-y^4,\\
F_6&=xy(x^4-y^4),\\
G_8&=x^8+14x^4y^4+y^8,\\
H_{12}&=x^{12}-33x^8y^4-33x^4y^8+y^{12},\\
A_4&=x^4+2i\sqrt3x^2y^2+y^4,\\
I_{12}&=xy(x^{10}+11x^5y^5-y^{10}),\\
I_{20}&=x^{20}-228x^{15}y^5+494x^{10}y^{10}+228x^5y^{15}+y^{20},\\
I_{30}&=x^{30}+522x^{25}y^5-10005x^{20}y^{10}
-10005x^{10}y^{20}-522x^5y^{25}+y^{30}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Each row means the free module generated by the listed secondaries over the
polynomial ring in the two primaries. Only monomials of total degree \(N\)
are retained.

| Sector | Primaries | Secondaries | Hilbert series in qubit variable \(z\) | Guaranteed order |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Quaternion fixed | \(Q_4,R_4\) | \(1,F_6\) | \((1+z^6)/(1-z^4)^2\) | 1 |
| Quaternion character | \(Q_4,R_4\) | \(U_2,V_4\) | \((z^2+z^4)/(1-z^4)^2\) | 1 |
| Tetrahedral fixed | \(F_6,G_8\) | \(1,H_{12}\) | \((1+z^{12})/((1-z^6)(1-z^8))\) | 2 |
| Tetrahedral character | \(F_6,G_8\) | \(A_4,\overline A_4^2\) | \((z^4+z^8)/((1-z^6)(1-z^8))\) | 2 |
| Octahedral fixed | \(G_8,F_6^2\) | \(1,F_6H_{12}\) | \((1+z^{18})/((1-z^8)(1-z^{12}))\) | 3 |
| Octahedral sign | \(G_8,F_6^2\) | \(F_6,H_{12}\) | \((z^6+z^{12})/((1-z^8)(1-z^{12}))\) | 3 |
| Icosahedral fixed | \(I_{12},I_{20}\) | \(1,I_{30}\) | \((1+z^{30})/((1-z^{12})(1-z^{20}))\) | 5 |

The relations checked directly in the construction include

\[
\begin{aligned}
F_6^2&=Q_4(R_4^2-4Q_4^2),\\
H_{12}^2&=G_8^3-108F_6^4,\\
I_{30}^2&=I_{20}^3+1728I_{12}^5,\\
A_4\overline A_4&=G_8.
\end{aligned}
\]

The quadratic relations explain why only zero or one copy of the secondary
generator is needed in the fixed sectors. Counting arbitrary powers of all
three basic forms would overcount the dimension. The two tetrahedral
nontrivial characters are complex conjugate; only one is included. The three
quaternion nontrivial character sectors are related by rotations; only one is
included.

## Sources

- Pereira and Paul-Paddock, [*Anticoherent Subspaces*](https://arxiv.org/html/1610.05841v2), invariant-polynomial construction and orders guaranteed by the symmetry group. The first-order maximality corollary in that paper is not used.
- Suter, [*Quantum affine Cartan matrices, Poincaré series of binary polyhedral groups, and reflection representations*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0503542), invariant Hilbert series and binary-polyhedral/ADE correspondence.
- Crass, [*Critically-Finite Dynamics on the Icosahedron*](https://doi.org/10.3390/sym12010177), explicit icosahedral forms; the signs here are obtained by replacing \(x\) by \(-x\) and rescaling the degree-12 form.
- OEIS [A028242](https://oeis.org/A028242), [A008612](https://oeis.org/A008612), [A008647](https://oeis.org/A008647), [A008651](https://oeis.org/A008651). These sequences use the spin index \(N/2\), omitting the zero odd-degree terms.
