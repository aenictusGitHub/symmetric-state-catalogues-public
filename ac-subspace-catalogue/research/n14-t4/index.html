Two rotation-inequivalent branches

The state and its complex conjugate both have proper rotational symmetry group . The nonzero rank-five component rules out any larger polyhedral group, and the nonzero Dicke support fixes the maximal axial rotation order at three. Thus the threefold axis is unique.
