\title{A universal separated-triple construction of second-order anticoherent subspaces}
\author{}
\date{}
\maketitle

Write $K(N,2)$ for the largest dimension of a subspace of the spin-$N/2$
representation all of whose unit vectors are second-order anticoherent.
The following is a constructive lower bound; it does not assert general
optimality.

\begin{theorem}
For every integer $N\ge6$,
\[
 K(N,2)\ge \left\lfloor\frac{N+3}{9}\right\rfloor.
\]
More explicitly, set
\[
 j=\frac N2,\qquad V=\frac{j(j+1)}3,\qquad
 k=\left\lfloor\frac{N+3}{9}\right\rfloor,\qquad
 h=\left\lfloor\frac{N-3(k-1)}2\right\rfloor.
\]
For $i=0,\ldots,k-1$, put
\[
 a_i=j-3i,\qquad b_i=a_i-h,\qquad c_i=a_i-2h,
\]
and
\[
 w_{i,0}=\frac{V+b_ic_i}{2h^2},\qquad
 w_{i,1}=-\frac{V+a_ic_i}{h^2},\qquad
 w_{i,2}=\frac{V+a_ib_i}{2h^2}.
\]
Then the vectors
\[
 \ket{u_i}=
 \sqrt{w_{i,0}}\ket{D_N^{(3i)}}+
 \sqrt{w_{i,1}}\ket{D_N^{(3i+h)}}+
 \sqrt{w_{i,2}}\ket{D_N^{(3i+2h)}}
\]
are an orthonormal basis of a second-order anticoherent subspace.  All the
weights are strictly positive rational numbers.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
We use the convention $J_z\ket{D_N^{(r)}}=(j-r)\ket{D_N^{(r)}}$.
First, $N\ge9k-3$, hence $h\ge3k$.  With $d=3(k-1)$, the integer
\[
 \epsilon=N-2h-d
\]
is either zero or one.  The support indices are the three blocks
\[
 \{0,3,\ldots,d\},\qquad
 \{h,h+3,\ldots,h+d\},\qquad
 \{2h,2h+3,\ldots,2h+d\}.
\]
Each is contained in $\{0,\ldots,N\}$, and the gap between consecutive
indices in their union is at least three, including between the blocks,
since $h-d\ge3$.

For equally spaced points $a,b=a-h,c=a-2h$, the displayed weights are the
unique solution of
\[
 w_0+w_1+w_2=1,\quad aw_0+bw_1+cw_2=0,\quad
 a^2w_0+b^2w_1+c^2w_2=V.
\]
These identities follow directly by substitution, or by Lagrange interpolation
of polynomials of degree at most two.

We verify positivity.  Since $a-b=b-c=h$, both products $ab$ and $bc$ are
at least $-h^2/4$.  Moreover,
\[
 \frac{h^2}{4}\le\frac{N^2}{16}<\frac{N(N+2)}{12}=V,
\]
so the outside weights are positive.  For the middle weight, the function
\[
 -a_ic_i=(j-3i)(2h-j+3i)
\]
is concave in $i$.  Its minimum is therefore attained at an endpoint.
At the two endpoints its values are
\[
 j(j-d-\epsilon),\qquad (j-d)(j-\epsilon),
\]
respectively; their difference is $d\epsilon\ge0$.  Consequently
\begin{align*}
 -a_ic_i-V
 &\ge j(j-d-\epsilon)-\frac{j(j+1)}3\\
 &=\frac j3\left(N-9k+8-3\epsilon\right)>0,
\end{align*}
because $N\ge9k-3$ and $\epsilon\le1$.  Thus the middle weights are also
strictly positive.

The moment identities and disjoint supports give, for the projector $P$ onto
the span of these vectors,
\[
 PJ_zP=0,\qquad PJ_z^2P=VP.
\]
Every monomial appearing in $J_+$, $J_+^2$, or
$J_zJ_++J_+J_z$ shifts the Dicke excitation index by one or two.  The global
support gap therefore gives
\[
 PJ_+P=PJ_+^2P=P(J_zJ_++J_+J_z)P=0,
\]
and the adjoint identities hold as well.  Using
$J_x^2+J_y^2+J_z^2=j(j+1)I$, these equations imply
\[
 PJ_\alpha P=0,\qquad
 P\frac{J_\alpha J_\beta+J_\beta J_\alpha}{2}P
 =\frac{j(j+1)}3\delta_{\alpha\beta}P,
 \qquad \alpha,\beta\in\{x,y,z\}.
\]
These are precisely the first- and second-order anticoherence compression
conditions.  They hold for every vector in the subspace, including every
superposition of the basis vectors.
\end{proof}

For example, this gives $K(35,2)\ge4$ and $K(42,2)\ge5$.
The corresponding Dicke indices and rational squared amplitudes are recorded
in \texttt{separated-triples-bases.json}.  The accompanying verifier checks all
conditions using exact rational arithmetic for $6\le N\le1000$; these finite
checks supplement the all-$N$ proof above.
