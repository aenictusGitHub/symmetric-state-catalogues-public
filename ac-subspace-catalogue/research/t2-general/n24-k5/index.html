Five residue classes, paired by reflection

Use normalized Dicke states. Unitary reversal pairs the first and fifth vectors and the second and fourth; the middle vector is palindromic.

There is no complex conjugation in the reversal. The coefficients are exact algebraic numbers specified below. A shorter expression in radicals has not been established.
