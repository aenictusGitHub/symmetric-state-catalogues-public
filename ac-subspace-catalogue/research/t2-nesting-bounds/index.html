Allowing broader first-order parents produces exact five- and six-dimensional examples at 35 and 42 qubits. A revised radical construction also improves the general four-band lower bound by two dimensions for every N at least 40.

Nonnegative integer-polynomial coefficient certificates prove positivity and strict quadrilateral feasibility in all eight remainder classes and for every q at least five. This is an all-N proof; the supplementary numerical checks validate the implementation only.

Every four-level row has two positive and two negative magnetic levels. Its mean-zero line extends analytically to a two-dimensional neutral plane. Their disjoint, same-parity direct sum is a 2k-dimensional first-order AC parent containing the constructed k-dimensional second-order subspace.

The previous four-band guarantee was floor(N/8) − 3. The stronger constant raises the combined general lower bound, for example, from eleven to twelve at N = 104. Its asymptotic coefficient remains 1/8, below the current upper-bound coefficient 1/5.
