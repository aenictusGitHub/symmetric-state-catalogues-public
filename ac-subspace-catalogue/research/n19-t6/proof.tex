\begin{theorem}
There is no pure sixth-order anticoherent state in
$V_{19/2}=\Sym^{19}(\mathbb C^2)$. Consequently
\[
 \boxed{K(19,t)=0\quad(t\ge6).}
\]
\end{theorem}
The exclusion is a finite exact certificate proof for arbitrary complex
states. Its final contradiction involves only the trace and six nonnegative
total-spin probabilities of a three-copy moment. No Majorana symmetry,
reality condition, or restricted Dicke support is assumed.

\section*{1. A necessary three-copy moment}
Suppose a normalized pure state $\psi\in V_{19/2}$ is sixth-order AC. Average its
three-copy projector over collective rotations:
\[
 X=\int_{\mathrm{SU}(2)}D_{19/2}(g)^{\otimes3}
 (|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|)^{\otimes3}
 D_{19/2}(g)^{\dagger\otimes3}\,dg.
\]
Then $X\succeq0$, $\Tr X=1$, and $X$ is invariant and supported on
\[
 W=\Sym^3(V_{19/2}),\qquad \dim W=\binom{22}{3}=1540.
\]
Before averaging, contraction of the first factor with any tensor of ranks
one through six gives zero, because its expectation in $\psi$ vanishes.
Rotation mixes components of a fixed tensor rank and preserves this zero
condition. Thus, in particular,
\begin{equation}\label{contractions}
 \Tr_1[(T_{\ell0}\otimes I^{\otimes2})X]=0,
 \qquad \ell=1,\ldots,6.
\end{equation}
These are necessary conditions; it is not asserted that every invariant
positive operator satisfying them is a physical three-copy state.

\section*{2. Complete exact invariant coordinates}
Decompose
\[
 W=\bigoplus_J V_J\otimes\mathbb C^{m_J}.
\]
There are 27 nonempty total-spin sectors. The accompanying certificate gives
complete integer highest-weight bases, with the multiplicities verified
independently from occupation-word weight counts. They satisfy
\[
 \sum_J(2J+1)m_J=1540,\qquad
 \sum_J\frac{m_J(m_J+1)}2=94.
\]
The coupled bases and the component-zero contraction equations are real.
For complex Hermitian multiplicity matrices, replacing each matrix by its
real part preserves positivity, the trace, and every selected equation.
Therefore it suffices to work with 94 real symmetric invariant coordinates.
This reduction does not assume that the original state has real amplitudes.

Here are the physical conventions needed to reconstruct these coordinates.
Use $b_i=\sqrt{\binom{19}{i}}|D_{19}^{(i)}\rangle$, $0\le i\le19$, and
the averaged symmetric words
\[
 E_{abc}=\frac1{3!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_3}
 b_{i_{\sigma(1)}}\otimes b_{i_{\sigma(2)}}\otimes b_{i_{\sigma(3)}},
 \qquad (i_1,i_2,i_3)=(a,b,c),\quad a\le b\le c.
\]
For occupation numbers $o_i$, their mutually orthogonal metric is
\[
 \|E_{abc}\|^2=\frac{\prod_i\binom{19}{i}^{o_i}o_i!}{3!}.
\]
Let $H_J$ be the certificate's full-column-rank integer highest-weight
matrix, orthogonal in this physical metric, and define
\[
 U_{J,M}=\frac{J_-^{J-M}}{(J-M)!}H_J,\qquad
 U_{J,M}^{\dagger}U_{J,M}=\binom{2J}{J-M}G_J,
 \quad G_J=H_J^{\dagger}H_J\succ0.
\]
For the supplied positive denominator scales $d_J$, every real invariant
operator has the unique form
\[
 X(B)=\sum_J d_J\sum_{M=-J}^{J}
 \frac{U_{J,M}B_JU_{J,M}^{\dagger}}{\binom{2J}{J-M}},
 \qquad B_J=B_J^T.
\]
Positivity is equivalent to $B_J\succeq0$ for every block. The upper
triangular entries of the $B_J$ form the 94 coordinates $b$; an off-diagonal
coordinate contributes both transposed entries.

The diagonal tensors $T_{\ell0}$ are represented by the degree-$\ell$
polynomials in $2i-19$ orthogonal to every smaller degree under the uniform
sum over $i=0,\ldots,19$. Selected matrix entries of
\eqref{contractions}, divided by each row's positive integer gcd, give
\[
 A b=0,\qquad A\in\mathbb Z^{88\times94}.
\]
The verifier reconstructs every selected row from the physical tensor
words. It checks $\operatorname{rank}A=88$ by elimination modulo the prime
$1000003$. The certificate's integer matrix $K$ satisfies
\[
 AK=0,\qquad K\in\mathbb Z^{94\times6},\qquad
 \operatorname{rank}K=6.
\]
The exact matrix product and the latter modular rank establish that $K$
spans the complete rational kernel. Hence every real solution is $b=Kz$.
Completeness of the selected contraction equations is not needed: every
hypothetical state satisfies this necessary subset.

\section*{3. A positive trace--probability identity}
Let $\Pi_J$ project onto the total-spin-$J$ sector of the three-copy space,
including all multiplicities, and put
\[
 p_J=\Tr(\Pi_JX).
\]
For a normalized positive moment, $p_J\ge0$ and $\sum_Jp_J=1$.
In the above exact coordinates these physical functionals are
\[
 p_J=d_J(2J+1)\Tr(G_JB_J),\qquad \Tr X=\sum_Jp_J.
\]
They can also be reconstructed by summing the physical trace over every
descendant $U_{J,M}$; the independent verifier checks the normalization.

Substitution of the complete kernel $b=Kz$ gives the following identity
for every solution of the selected necessary equations:
\begin{align*}
 0={}&1413987969381349416781077855914791923\,\Tr X\\
 &+9858033828239721287105637920113568802000\,p_{3/2}\\
 &+7713415871370069955275107921621388912000\,p_{5/2}\\
 &+1538043828417702353592041548624293431250\,p_{15/2}\\
 &+1482076991122688830274198406278368150000\,p_{17/2}\\
 &+683439424711170559646778746197339968000\,p_{19/2}\\
 &+1122458021446107869346053838715358328000\,p_{39/2}.
\end{align*}
The identity is checked coefficientwise in all six kernel parameters with
exact rational arithmetic. Every displayed coefficient is strictly
positive. Since $\Tr X=1$ and the six probabilities are nonnegative, its
right side is strictly positive, a contradiction. Thus the assumed
sixth-order AC state cannot exist. Higher orders are excluded by nesting.
