\title{An explicit radical third-order AC plane for twenty qubits}
\author{Catalogue calculation}\date{3 November 2026}\maketitle
We construct an orthonormal pair whose entire complex span is third-order
anticoherent. All Dicke states are normalized and square roots are the
nonnegative real square roots. This is an independent analytical witness;
no identification with a rounded numerical plane in \texttt{ACLiege.jl} is
asserted.

\section{Explicit coefficients}
Let $R_r=4r$ for $0\leq r\leq5$ and $S_r=3+4r$ for $0\leq r\leq4$.
Choose
\[
 p=(3927,333,9318,5018,2483,3497)/24576,\qquad
 q=(143,23,53,117,48)/384.
\]
Set
\[
 U=\frac{89947}{262144},\qquad
 V=-\frac{274443}{1048576},\qquad W=\frac{26039}{393216},
\]
\[
 A=\sqrt U,\qquad B=\frac{V+i\sqrt{4UW-V^2}}{2\sqrt U},
 \qquad Z_r=(-1)^r\binom4r(A+rB).
\]
For $0\leq r\leq4$ let
\[
 f_r=\sqrt{(20-S_r)(S_r+1)},\qquad
 g_r=\sqrt{\prod_{h=0}^{2}(20-R_r-h)(R_r+h+1)},\qquad
 L_r=g_r\sqrt{p_rq_r}.
\]
Define
\[
 C=-L_0+L_1+L_2,\qquad
 x=-\frac{C^2+L_3^2-L_4^2}{2C},\qquad y=\sqrt{L_3^2-x^2},
\]
\[
 (T_0,T_1,T_2,T_3,T_4)=(-L_0,L_1,L_2,x+iy,-C-x-iy).
\]
Finally set
\[
 a_0=\sqrt{p_0},\qquad
 a_{r+1}=\frac{g_rZ_r}{f_rT_r}a_r,\qquad
 b_r=\overline{\frac{Z_r}{f_ra_{r+1}}}\quad(0\leq r\leq4).
\]
An explicit basis is
\[
 |u\rangle=\sum_{r=0}^{5}a_r\D{4r},\qquad
 |v\rangle=\sum_{r=0}^{4}b_r\D{3+4r}.
\]
This finite recurrence is an expression in radicals, with no free parameters.
The file \texttt{basis.json} also gives every expanded component.

\section{Well-defined radicals and exact norms}
First,
\[
 4UW-V^2=\frac{73835750677}{3298534883328}>0.
\]
Thus $|A+rB|^2=U+rV+r^2W$. Direct rational substitution proves
\[
 |Z_r|^2=\binom4r^2(U+rV+r^2W)=f_r^2p_{r+1}q_r.
\]
The other squared lengths are the positive rationals
\[
 (L_0^2,L_1^2,L_2^2,L_3^2,L_4^2)=
 \left(\frac{160044885}{65536},\frac{9382275}{16384},
 \frac{2240862525}{32768},\frac{934966305}{16384},
 \frac{7218081}{4096}\right).
\]
Comparing these rationals with integer squares proves
\[
 49<L_0<50,\quad23<L_1<24,\quad261<L_2<262,
 \quad238<L_3<239,\quad41<L_4<42.
\]
In particular $234<C<237$ and
\[
 |L_3-L_4|<198<234<C<237<279<L_3+L_4.
\]
The strict triangle inequalities imply $y^2>0$. More explicitly,
\[
 4C^2y^2=((L_3+L_4)^2-C^2)(C^2-(L_3-L_4)^2)>0.
\]
The construction therefore gives
\[
 \sum_{r=0}^{4}T_r=0,\qquad |T_r|^2=L_r^2=g_r^2p_rq_r.
\]
Starting from $|a_0|^2=p_0$, the recurrence proves by induction
\[
 |a_r|^2=p_r,\qquad |b_r|^2=q_r,
\]
\[
 f_ra_{r+1}\overline{b_r}=Z_r,\qquad
 g_ra_r\overline{b_r}=T_r.
\]
All $p_r,q_r$ are strictly positive; hence no denominator vanishes.
The supports are disjoint and both probability lists sum to one, proving
orthonormality.

\section{Third-order AC for every superposition}
Exact rational moment identities are
\[
 \sum_r p_r(4r)^d=\sum_r q_r(3+4r)^d
 =\frac1{21}\sum_{j=0}^{20}j^d,\qquad0\leq d\leq3.
\]
Distinct occupied Dicke indices within each vector differ by at least four,
so all self off-diagonal moments of rank at most three vanish. The four
diagonal identities therefore give both three-qubit self reductions $I_4/4$.

The only cross-support differences of absolute value at most three are
$R_{r+1}-S_r=1$ and $R_r-S_r=-3$. For the former, all rank-one, rank-two and
rank-three conditions reduce to
\[
 \sum_{r=0}^{4}r^dZ_r=0\qquad(d=0,1,2).
\]
Indeed the fourth finite difference annihilates every polynomial of degree
at most three, and $r^d(A+rB)$ has degree at most three. For the latter the
sole rank-three condition is $\sum T_r=0$, already proved. Their conjugates
give the adjoint moments. Consequently every ordered three-qubit reduction is
\[
 R_3(|u_i\rangle\langle u_j|)=\delta_{ij}\frac{I_4}{4}.
\]
This is the whole-subspace condition, not merely separate AC conditions on
the two basis states.

\section{Independent verification and optimality}
The primary verifier reconstructs the saved radical coefficients and checks
all forty ordered spin-moment entries $J_+^qJ_z^p$, $q+p\leq3$.
The independent verifier imports no construction code. It separately checks
the radical recurrence, its exact norm identities and rational positivity
bounds. It then constructs all sixty-four physical reduced entries from
\[
 R_3(|u\rangle\langle v|)_{ab}
 =\sum_{h=0}^{17}u_{a+h}\overline{v_{b+h}}
 \frac{\sqrt{\binom3a\binom3b}\binom{17}h}
 {\sqrt{\binom{20}{a+h}\binom{20}{b+h}}}.
\]
For cross entries it substitutes the certified products $Z_r/f_r$ and
$T_r/g_r$ before simplification. The vanishing is a polynomial identity in
the phase variables, so no large primitive algebraic field needs to be
expanded. Every exported coefficient is exactly associated with the
verified radical recurrence; no decimal tolerance is used in the AC proof.

The separately certified spin-$5/2$ cubic-inertia bound gives
$6k\leq N-4$ at $N=20$, hence $k\leq2$. Combining it with this witness yields
\[
 \boxed{K(20,3)=2.}
\]
The upper-bound proof is supplied in
\texttt{../t3-spin5half-inertia/proof.tex}.
