\author{Exact catalogue calculation}

\begin{theorem}
No pure spin-$7$ state is fifth-order anticoherent. Consequently,
\[
\boxed{K(14,t)=0\quad(t\ge5).}
\]
The existing exact fourth-order state therefore attains the maximum
pure-state AC order at fourteen qubits.
\end{theorem}

The proof below is finite exact linear algebra, with an explicit positivity
certificate. The accompanying \texttt{certificate.json} and standard-library
Python verifier give all integer matrices and reconstruct the equations
independently of the discovery implementation.

\paragraph{1. Necessary three-copy equations.}
Suppose a normalized $\psi\in V_7$ is fifth-order AC. With
$\rho=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$, define
\[
X=\int_{\mathrm{SU}(2)}(U\rho U^\dagger)^{\otimes3}\,dU.
\]
Then $X\succeq0$, $\Tr X=1$, and $X$ is invariant on the
$\binom{17}{3}=680$ dimensional space $\operatorname{Sym}^3(V_7)$.
Anticoherence of every rotated state implies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:necessary}
\Tr_1[(T_{\ell0}\otimes I\otimes I)X]=0,
\qquad 1\le\ell\le5.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{2. Exact invariant coordinates and positivity.}
Use $b_i=\sqrt{\binom{14}{i}}|i\rangle$, $m=i-7$, and let $E_o$ be
the average of the distinct three-letter words with occupations $o_i$.
Then
\[
\|E_o\|^2=\frac{\prod_i\binom{14}{i}^{o_i}o_i!}{3!}.
\]
The raising and lowering coefficients in this basis are respectively
$o_i(i+1)$ and $o_i(15-i)$. The highest-weight integer matrices $H_J$
in the certificate generate descendants
\[
U_{J,M}=\frac{J_-^{J-M}}{(J-M)!}H_J.
\]
The spin multiplicities, listed as $(J,m_J)$, are
\[
\begin{gathered}
(1,1),(3,2),(4,1),(5,2),(6,2),(7,3),(8,2),(9,3),\\
(10,2),(11,2),(12,2),(13,2),(14,1),(15,2),\\
(16,1),(17,1),(18,1),(19,1),(21,1).
\end{gathered}
\]
They satisfy $\sum_J(2J+1)m_J=680$. The independent verifier checks
their distinct spins, highest-weight equations, column independence,
ladder relations and completeness.

An invariant Hermitian operator has the form
\[
X=\sum_J d_J\sum_{M=-J}^{J}
\frac{U_{J,M} B_J U_{J,M}^{\dagger}}
{\binom{2J}{J-M}},
\]
where the displayed expression denotes coefficients of physical
$|E_o\rangle\langle E_{o'}|$, $d_J>0$ clears binomial denominators,
and $B_J$ is Hermitian. For each $J$, the normalized ladder embeddings
are full column rank. Their Gram matrix is the same positive definite
matrix for every $M$. Thus this is an injective congruence of
$I_{2J+1}\otimes B_J$, and $X\succeq0$ implies $B_J\succeq0$.

All bases and equations used in \eqref{eq:necessary} are real. Taking
entrywise real parts of the $B_J$ preserves these equations, their
physical trace, and positivity on real vectors. Consequently an
arbitrary complex state would give real symmetric positive semidefinite
$B_J$ satisfying the equations and normalization. This step does not
assume real state amplitudes.

\paragraph{3. The exact two-dimensional kernel.}
There are $\sum_Jm_J(m_J+1)/2=47$ independent real coordinates,
collected in $\mathbf b$. The diagonal rank-$\ell$ tensors may be
scaled to discrete orthogonal polynomials in $z=2i-14$:
\[
\begin{aligned}
f_1&=z/2,&f_2&=(3z^2-224)/4,\\
f_3&=z(5z^2-668)/48,&
f_4&=(35z^4-6620z^2+145152)/192,\\
f_5&=z(63z^4-15260z^2+708032)/1920.
\end{aligned}
\]
They are reconstructed by exact Gram--Schmidt orthogonalization on the
fifteen magnetic eigenvalues. Contracting the first letter gives
$(o_i/3)\sqrt{\binom{14}{i}}E_{o-e_i}$, so every equation in
\eqref{eq:necessary} has rational coefficients.

The certificate selects $45$ primitive integer equations
$A\mathbf b=0$ with $A\in\mathbb Z^{45\times47}$. The verifier
reconstructs them and verifies rank $45$ modulo the prime $1000003$.
It also verifies an integer matrix $R\in\mathbb Z^{47\times2}$ with
$AR=0$ and rank $2$. Therefore every solution is exactly
\[
\mathbf b=R\binom{u}{v}.
\]

\paragraph{4. Three diagonal inequalities give a contradiction.}
Take the first diagonal of the $J=1$ block, the second diagonal of
the $J=3$ block, and the first diagonal of the $J=5$ block. Substituting
the kernel and dividing each coefficient row by its positive gcd gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
L_1(u,v)&=1094490516902169308u-125072808123773475v,\\
L_2(u,v)&=-42202464514105998136u+2086674682719126039v,\\
L_3(u,v)&=-11877362318408u+1509722697267v.
\end{aligned}
\]
Positive semidefiniteness requires $L_1,L_2,L_3\ge0$. But the exact
integer identity
\[
\boxed{700L_1+3L_2+53844949L_3=0}
\]
forces all three to vanish. These forms have rank two, hence $u=v=0$.
Thus every positive semidefinite solution of the necessary equations
is $X=0$, contradicting $\Tr X=1$. This proves the theorem.

\paragraph{Direct contradiction using physical probabilities.}
Write $p_J=\Tr(\Pi_JX)$ for the physical total-spin-$J$ probability
of the three-copy moment. The complete constraint kernel satisfies
\[
\boxed{265256992\Tr X+26961161975p_4+118909381500p_{16}=0.}
\]
All coefficients are strictly positive. Because $\Tr X=1$ and
$p_4,p_{16}\ge0$, the left side is strictly positive, a contradiction.
The verifier reconstructs the physical trace by summing every word of
every spin block and verifies this identity exactly. This is a direct
physical-probability version of the exclusion.

\paragraph{Equivalent certificate using physical probabilities.}
The same two-dimensional kernel yields a basis-independent version of
the contradiction. Write $p_J=\Tr(\Pi_J X)$ for the physical total-spin-$J$
probability of the three-copy moment. Then
\[
\boxed{21094337p_4+4375800p_{14}+119335580p_{16}=0.}
\]
All three probabilities are nonnegative, so they vanish. Their linear
forms on the two-dimensional kernel have rank two, again forcing
$\mathbf b=0$ and contradicting normalization. To verify the physical
normalization, the checker reconstructs each probability by summing
the occupation-word norms over every descendant. It independently
checks the highest-weight formula
\[
p_J=d_J(2J+1)\Tr\!\left(H_J^T G_JH_J B_J\right),
\]
where $G_J$ is the diagonal occupation-word Gram matrix at weight $J$.
Both the probability identity and its rank-two property are checked
exactly in the accompanying verifier.

\paragraph{Checks and consequences.}
The exclusion propagates to all $t\ge5$ by nesting. The independently
certified fourth-order radical state at $N=14$ proves that the maximum
pure-state order is four. As a separate positive control, the exact
icosahedral fifth-order state at $N=12$ has $\rho_5=I_6/6$ exactly;
its three-copy invariant moment satisfies the same construction
numerically, with trace one and contraction residual below $10^{-15}$.
This numerical control is not used as a premise of the exact exclusion.
