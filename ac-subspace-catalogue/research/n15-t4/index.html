Fifteen qubits cannot be fourth-order anticoherent

Proved · arbitrary complex pure states

A four-copy moment and its partial transpose must both be positive. An exact rational certificate shows that these positivity conditions contradict fourth-order anticoherence. No constellation symmetry or restricted Dicke support is assumed.

The complete answer at orders two, three and four

The existing exact algebraic three-dimensional AC2 subspace is optimal. A separate four-copy certificate excludes every AC3 plane, and a four-component Dicke-basis superposition attains order three. The present proof excludes order four, and hence every higher order.
