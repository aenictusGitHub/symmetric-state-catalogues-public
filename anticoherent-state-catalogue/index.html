02 / Reading the catalogue

What is anticoherence?

A pure symmetric state is t-anticoherent when its spin moments up to order t are independent of direction. Equivalently, its t-qubit reduced state is maximally mixed within the symmetric subspace:

The symmetric subspace has dimension t + 1. This condition refers to that subspace, rather than the entire t-qubit Hilbert space.

All ranks, through order t

Every spin multipole of rank 1 through t must vanish. Cancelling only even ranks is insufficient for full anticoherence.

A t-AC state is also t′-AC for each t′ ≤ t. The order shown beside a representative is the order verified for that particular state.
