\title{Twenty-three qubits: certified exact fifth-order anticoherence}
\author{Rational reduced-state equations and an isolating contraction}
\date{5 November 2026}\maketitle
\begin{theorem}
An exactly defined algebraic pure symmetric twenty-three-qubit state is
fifth-order anticoherent. Together with the independent universal sixth-order
exclusion in \texttt{../n23-t6/proof.tex}, this proves
\[\boxed{t_{\max}(23)=5.}\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The state is defined by an isolated root of rational quadratic equations.
The recorded decimal Dicke components are approximations; closed-form radical
expressions are not claimed.

Let $d_r$ be the 24 positive integer scales in \texttt{certificate.json}.
For $x\in\mathbb R^{48}$ put
\[
z_r=x_r+ix_{24+r},\qquad
c_r=\frac{\sqrt{\binom{23}{r}}}{d_r}z_r,\qquad
|\psi(x)\rangle=\sum_{r=0}^{23}c_r|D_{23}^{(r)}\rangle.
\]
The Dicke kets are normalized. For $0\le a\le5$ and $0\le\ell\le18$, the
five-qubit Schmidt matrix is
\[
M_{a\ell}=\frac{\sqrt{\binom5a\binom{18}\ell}}{d_{a+\ell}}z_{a+\ell}.
\]
The identity $MM^\dagger=I_6/6$ is equivalent to the following 36 real
rational quadratic equations $F(x)=0$: six diagonal equations
\[
\sum_{\ell=0}^{18}\frac{\binom{18}\ell}{d_{a+\ell}^2}
\bigl(x_{a+\ell}^2+x_{24+a+\ell}^2\bigr)
-\frac1{6\binom5a}=0,
\]
and the real and imaginary parts of 15 upper entries
\[
\sum_{\ell=0}^{18}\frac{\binom{18}\ell}{d_{a+\ell}d_{b+\ell}}
z_{a+\ell}\overline{z_{b+\ell}}=0\qquad(0\le a<b\le5).
\]
Their order is the six diagonal equations, followed by real and imaginary
parts of upper entries in lexicographic order.

The certificate records a rational center $x^0$, 36 active coordinates and
12 fixed coordinates. Fix the latter to their rational central values and
write $y\in\mathbb R^{36}$ for the former. This is a construction choice,
not a restriction imposed on every AC state.
A rational $36\times36$ matrix $A$, whose denominator is $2^{256}$, satisfies
the following exactly checked bounds at $J_0=DF(y^0)$:
\[
\eta=\|AF(y^0)\|_\infty<4\cdot10^{-66},\qquad
\beta=\|I-AJ_0\|_\infty<10^{-76},\qquad
\|A\|_\infty<1600.
\]
Take $r=10^{-65}$. Each equation has two quadratic monomials per $\ell$.
The sum of absolute Hessian entries is bounded by
\[
H\le4\sum_{\ell=0}^{18}\binom{18}\ell=2^{20},
\]
since all scales are at least one. Thus the map $T(y)=y-AF(y)$ on the closed
infinity-norm radius-$r$ box satisfies
\[
\|DT(y)\|_\infty\le q=\beta+\|A\|_\infty Hr<10^{-55}<1,
\qquad \|T(y)-y^0\|_\infty\le\eta+qr<r.
\]
The full rational inequalities are verified directly; their displayed
decimals are not proof premises. Banach's theorem supplies a unique fixed point
$y^*$ in the box. Since $\beta<1$, the square matrix $AJ_0$ and hence $A$ are
invertible. Therefore $F(y^*)=0$. The derivative bound also makes
$DF(y^*)$ invertible, so this is an isolated nonsingular zero of a rational
polynomial system. Its coordinates are real algebraic numbers.

The exact root, together with the 12 fixed rational coordinates, specifies
the state precisely. Its reduction is $\rho_5=I_6/6$; tracing this identity
proves exact normalization. Because
$d_r^2\le\binom{23}{r}<4d_r^2$, each real or imaginary Dicke component is
within $2\cdot10^{-65}$ of the exact rational-center conversion.
Binary floating values and plotted Majorana points are numerical renderings.

The separate exact four-copy positivity certificate excludes every
sixth-order pure symmetric state on 23 qubits. By nesting it excludes all
higher orders. Thus the certified state has exactly order five and attains
the global maximum.
\end{proof}
