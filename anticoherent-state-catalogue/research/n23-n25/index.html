23 qubits: certified maximum order five

The exact state is defined by a unique algebraic root near the refined Liège numerical state:

|\psi_{23}\rangle=\sum_{r=0}^{23}\frac{\sqrt{\binom{23}{r}}}{d_r}(x_r^*+ix_{24+r}^*)|D_{23}^{(r)}\rangle.

The certificate records 48 real coordinates, positive integer scales d_r, a rational center, and 12 coordinates fixed to their central values. The other 36 coordinates are the unique real zero in the stated box of the rational quadratic equations equivalent to \rho_5=I_6/6. This is an exact implicit algebraic definition; closed-form radical components are not claimed.

Existence from a rational contraction

For the 36 active variables, set T(y)=y-AF(y). Exact rational arithmetic verifies

\begin{gathered}r=10^{-65},\quad\|AF(y^0)\|_\infty<4\cdot10^{-66},\\\|I-ADF(y^0)\|_\infty<10^{-76},\quad\|A\|_\infty<1600,\\H\le2^{20},\qquad q<10^{-55},\qquad\|AF(y^0)\|_\infty+qr<r.\end{gathered}

Banach’s theorem supplies a unique fixed point in the box. The inverse-defect bound makes A invertible, so the fixed point satisfies F=0. Its nonsingular rational polynomial system makes the coordinates algebraic. The identity reduction proves exact normalization. Every real or imaginary Dicke component is within 2\cdot10^{-65} of the exact rational-center conversion. Floating components and copied nearby Majorana points are numerical renderings.

Why order six is impossible

An exact four-copy positivity certificate excludes every sixth-order pure symmetric state on 23 qubits. For a hypothetical state, its rotationally averaged four-copy projector is positive, has trace one, and satisfies necessary low-multipole contraction equations. A coefficientwise rational separating identity with positive-definite certificate matrices gives a contradiction. This applies to arbitrary complex states without restricting their support or constellation symmetry. Together with the certified fifth-order state, it proves the maximum five.

Why order five is globally maximal

An exact four-copy certificate excludes every sixth-order pure symmetric state.
