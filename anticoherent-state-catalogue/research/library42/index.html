Numerical library representatives

87 saved library representatives through N=42 were imported from the audited refinement of the original ACstates folder. Their reported orders were independently checked from the saved 85-digit Dicke components using 100-digit partial traces. Every reported order has Frobenius residual below 10⁻⁶⁰; every next order has residual above 0.00177. These are numerical checks, not exact existence or maximum-order proofs.

The original coordinate and Dicke files disagree in 16 records across the full library. The catalogue follows the audited coordinate-derived representatives and their refinements, rather than substituting an inconsistent raw Dicke file. Original files and the refinement audit remain linked in the Majorana-degeneracy catalogue.

Two unlisted candidates, acs.29.6_*.txt and acs.13.3.4.txt, fail their claimed orders and are not counted as witnesses. The older acs.37.6.txt state passes; it was independently refined and added as a matching sixth-order representative.

Exact lower-order examples

91 additional analytical states were constructed to match the selected lower order exactly. Together with existing representatives, these provide exact-order defaults whenever available. Where no matching-order construction is recorded, a higher-order representative is labelled by its own order.
