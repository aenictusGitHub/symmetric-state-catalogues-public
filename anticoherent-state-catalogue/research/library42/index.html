Every qubit number through N=100

The catalogue includes every row from N=1 to N=100, with orders 1–19. Numerical library states are shown in blue; exact constructions and proved exclusions remain distinct. An unfilled higher-order cell records missing evidence rather than a failed-search exclusion.

Numerical library representatives

153 saved library representatives through N=100 were imported from the audited refinement of the original ACstates folder and additional source checks. Their reported orders were independently checked from saved high-precision Dicke components using 100-digit partial traces. The largest Frobenius residual through a reported order is 6.4363e−66; the smallest residual at the next order is 4.2948e−5. These are numerical checks, not exact existence or maximum-order proofs.

The original coordinate and Dicke files disagree in some records. The catalogue follows the audited coordinate-derived representatives and their refinements, rather than substituting an inconsistent raw Dicke file. Original files and the refinement audit remain linked in the Majorana-degeneracy catalogue.

The unlisted candidates acs.29.6_*.txt and acs.13.3.4.txt fail their claimed orders and are not counted as witnesses. The file named acs.48.8.txt contains 50 Majorana coordinates and fails its claimed eighth order; it is not used as an eighth-order N=48 witness. Additional accepted and rejected source checks are recorded in the audit downloads.

The numerical N=60, order-10 representative is included with the same independent reduced-state checks. Its reported order is not a proof of maximality.

6 additional original local records were independently refined and checked at their reported orders. They provide numerical states at N=65,70,72,75,84,96, including higher observed orders at N=65,70,72,75. These are numerical observations, not exact existence or maximum-order proofs. Read the source audit.

Exact lower-order examples

499 additional analytical states were constructed to match the selected lower order exactly. Normalization, all required reductions and failure at the next order are checked with exact symbolic arithmetic. Together with existing representatives, these provide exact-order defaults whenever available. Where no matching-order construction is recorded, a higher-order representative is labelled by its own order.
