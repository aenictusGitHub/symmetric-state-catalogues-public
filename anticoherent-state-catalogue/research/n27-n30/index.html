27 qubits: an explicit fifth-order state

With normalized Dicke vectors, set

w=\frac{52-i\sqrt{3433}}{\sqrt{6137}}\sqrt{\frac{327}{2500}}\bigl(|D_{27}^{(1)}\rangle-|D_{27}^{(26)}\rangle\bigr)-i\sqrt{\frac{494}{2500}}\bigl(|D_{27}^{(6)}\rangle-|D_{27}^{(21)}\rangle\bigr)-iw\sqrt{\frac{429}{2500}}\bigl(|D_{27}^{(11)}\rangle-|D_{27}^{(16)}\rangle\bigr)

Independent exact direct partial traces give

\rho_5=I_6/6,\qquad(\rho_6)_{00}-1/7=\frac{17}{1593900}\ne0.

The order is exactly five, and all 27 Majorana points are distinct. A reciprocal-polynomial argument proves the multiplicities exactly.

The universal upper bound

A rigorously validated six-copy partial-transpose positivity certificate excludes every pure sixth-order state. Exact factorial contractions, positive Gram factors and outward-rounded interval bounds cover arbitrary complex amplitudes. Together with the exact fifth-order witness, this proves t_{\max}(27)=5. No constellation-symmetry restriction is imposed.

29 qubits: an explicit fifth-order state

With normalized Dicke vectors, set

w=\frac{3094-i\sqrt{2170247}}{\sqrt{11743083}}\sqrt{\frac{3598}{18750}}\bigl(|D_{29}^{(2)}\rangle-|D_{29}^{(27)}\rangle\bigr)-i\sqrt{\frac{2081}{18750}}\bigl(|D_{29}^{(7)}\rangle-|D_{29}^{(22)}\rangle\bigr)-iw\sqrt{\frac{3696}{18750}}\bigl(|D_{29}^{(12)}\rangle-|D_{29}^{(17)}\rangle\bigr)

Independent exact direct partial traces give

\rho_5=I_6/6,\qquad(\rho_6)_{00}-1/7=- \frac{578}{1781325}\ne0.

The order is exactly five. This representative has two double Majorana poles and 25 simple finite points, giving the partition (2^2,1^{25}). It differs from the all-simple numerical library representative.

What the upper bound settles

Exact factorial contractions and outward-rounded interval bounds cover arbitrary complex amplitudes. The maximum is therefore 5\le t_{\max}(29)\le6. Sixth-order existence remains unresolved by this catalogue.

28 qubits: maximum order six

A rational contraction certificate defines a unique exact algebraic state near the refined Liège representative:

|\psi\rangle=\sum_{r=0}^{28}\frac{\sqrt{\binom{28}{r}}}{d_r}\bigl(x_r^*+ix_{29+r}^*\bigr)|D_{28}^{(r)}\rangle

The certificate fixes nine rational coordinates and isolates the remaining 49 coordinates by 49 rational quadratic equations equivalent to \rho_6=I_7/7. Its radius is 10^{-65}. An independent standard-library verifier reconstructs the equations, exact Jacobian and approximate-inverse defect, then proves strict self-mapping and contraction. The root has algebraic coordinates; closed-form radical components are not claimed.

\|AF(x^0)\|_\infty<10^{-66},\quad\|I-ADF(x^0)\|_\infty<10^{-76},\quad q<10^{-53}.

A rational interval also proves (\rho_7)_{00}-1/8<-1/40000 for this root. The displayed decimal components and nearby Majorana coordinates are numerical renderings of the exact definition.

The universal upper bound

A separate rigorously validated four-copy positivity certificate excludes every seventh-order pure symmetric state. This proves t_{\max}(28)=6 together with the exact sixth-order state.

30 qubits: maximum order seven

Two octahedral binary forms give a compact radical construction:

F_6=z(1-z^4),\qquad F_8=1+14z^4+z^8,\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{326271}}4,\quad\beta=\frac{-118635+14355i\sqrt{287}}{256\sqrt{326271}}c_r=\frac{[z^r]\bigl(\alpha F_6^5+\beta F_6F_8^3\bigr)}{\sqrt{\binom{30}r}},\qquad |\psi_{30}\rangle=\sum_{r=0}^{30}c_r|D_{30}^{(r)}\rangle.

Only eight Dicke components are nonzero. Exact Schmidt reductions and an independent direct partial trace prove

\rho_7=I_8/8,\qquad(\rho_8)_{00}-1/9=-\frac{11}{538200}\ne0.

All 30 Majorana points are distinct, proved by a polynomial gcd after extracting the six octahedral vertices.

The universal upper bound

An exact rational three-copy positivity certificate excludes every eighth-order pure symmetric state. The independent verifier reconstructs all physical contraction equations, the separating identity, and positive matrices. Hence t_{\max}(30)=7.
