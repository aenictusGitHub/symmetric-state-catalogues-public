\title{A six-copy exclusion of pure order-6 anticoherence\protect\\on 27 symmetric qubits}
\author{Portable validated certificate}\date{November 2026}\maketitle

\begin{theorem}
There is no normalized pure state of 27 symmetric qubits that is
anticoherent through order 6. Consequently the maximum pure-state
anticoherence order on 27 qubits is 5, using the exact order-5 witness
recorded at the end of this proof.
\end{theorem}

The exclusion is a finite separating identity, with positivity proved by
outward-rounded interval arithmetic. The optimizer used to discover its
coefficients is not a premise of the proof. A successful run of the
supplied \texttt{verify\_certificate.py} checks every representation and
positivity assertion used below. Until that complete run succeeds, the
stored numerical candidate alone does not establish this theorem.

\paragraph{The physical moment and its normalization.}
Put $j=27/2$ and $V=\Sym^{27}(\mathbb C^2)$, so $\dim V=28$.
For a hypothetical pure order-6 AC state $\rho=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$,
form the normalized Haar average
\[
 X=\int_{\mathrm{SU}(2)}(U_g\rho U_g^\dagger)^{\otimes6}\,dg.
\]
It has trace one, is positive, and is supported on $\Sym^6(V)$.
Throughout this certificate, capital spin labels $J,S,T,L$ are
\emph{doubled} spins: a spin-$J/2$ irrep has dimension $J+1$.
In orthonormal multiplicity coordinates, write
\[
 X=\bigoplus_J I_{J+1}\otimes\frac{C_J}{\sqrt{J+1}},
 \qquad C_J\succeq0,
 \qquad\sum_J\sqrt{J+1}\Tr C_J=1.                 \tag{1}
\]
There are 81 nonempty source sectors and 20,076 highest-weight columns.
Their dimensions sum to
$\dim\Sym^6(V)=\binom{33}{6}=1,107,568$.

Let $E_J$ be the isometric coefficients of fractional parentage (CFPs)
from the source multiplicity space into the coupled basis of
$V\otimes\Sym^5(V)$. Its row blocks $E_{J,S}$ are indexed by the
five-copy spin $S$ and its multiplicity. The admissible rows obey
$|27-S|\le J\le27+S$. All these bases can be chosen real.

\paragraph{Anticoherence equations.}
For $1\le\ell\le6$, let $f_\ell(i)$, $0\le i\le27$, be the diagonal
entries of the Frobenius-normalized rank-$\ell$, component-zero spin
tensor. They are constructed by exact Gram--Schmidt from
$(2i-27)^0,\ldots,(2i-27)^\ell$, with positive leading coefficient.
Anticoherence implies
\[
 \Tr_1[(\operatorname{diag}f_\ell\otimes I)X]=0.       \tag{2}
\]
Indeed the integrand factors into the vanishing rank-$\ell$ expectation
of $U_g\rho U_g^\dagger$ times its five-copy tensor power.
Rotation covariance reduces (2) to multiplicity matrices
\[
 R_{\ell,S,T}(C)
 =\sum_J c_{\ell,J,S,T}E_{J,S}C_JE_{J,T}^{T}=0.       \tag{3}
\]
For $S\le T$, the verifier evaluates the scalar directly as
\[
 c_{\ell,J,S,T}=
 \frac{\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{27}f_\ell(i)
 \langle j,m_i;S/2,S/2\mid J/2,S/2+m_i\rangle
 \langle j,m_i;T/2,S/2\mid J/2,S/2+m_i\rangle}
 {\sqrt{J+1}\,
  |\langle S/2,S/2;T/2,-S/2\mid\ell,0\rangle|},
 \quad m_i=i-j.                                     \tag{4}
\]
Forbidden triangles contribute zero. The absolute value in (4) fixes the
output tensor phase at its upper common weight. Exact factorial
Clebsch--Gordan sums, enclosed by Arb, avoid assuming an inferred phase
or a floating recoupling identity. The certificate pairs real dyadic
matrices with these equations; off-diagonal $(S,T)$ blocks are included
with their transpose. The 8,750,229 reduced scalar entries are not
claimed to be linearly independent. Only their necessary vanishing is
used.

\paragraph{A positive partial-time-reversal constraint.}
Define $F_j|j,m\rangle=(-1)^{j-m}|j,-m\rangle$ and
$\Theta=F_jK$, with $K$ complex conjugation in this basis. Partial
transpose on the first copy, followed by this unitary, gives
\[
 \tau_1(X)=(F_j\otimes I)X^{\Gamma_1}(F_j^\dagger\otimes I)
 =\int\Theta\rho_g\Theta^{-1}\otimes\rho_g^{\otimes5}\,dg\succeq0,
 \quad\rho_g=U_g\rho U_g^\dagger.                    \tag{5}
\]
This operator is supported on $V\otimes\Sym^5(V)$; it need not be
supported on $\Sym^6(V)$. Time reversal makes it rotation invariant.
Define its normalized output multiplicity blocks by
\[
 \tau_1(X)=\bigoplus_L I_{L+1}\otimes
                  \frac{Y_L}{\sqrt{L+1}},\qquad Y_L\succeq0.
\]
In the same first-spin/parent coupled convention as the source CFPs,
\[
 (Y_L)_{S\alpha,T\beta}
 =\sum_J\kappa_{L,J,S,T}
       (E_{J,S}C_JE_{J,T}^{T})_{\alpha\beta},\qquad
 \kappa_{L,J,S,T}
 =\sqrt{(J+1)(L+1)}
 \begin{Bmatrix}27/2&S/2&J/2\\27/2&T/2&L/2\end{Bmatrix}.           \tag{6}
\]
The partial-time-reversal recoupling phase is
$(-1)^{6\cdot27}=+1$ in this gauge. The Wigner $6j$ symbol is evaluated
from exact integer factorial sums with outward interval rounding.
The certificate uses only $L=0,2,\ldots,20$; unused output sectors need
not be tested.

\paragraph{The separating identity.}
For each used output spin, the saved binary matrix $F_L$ defines the
\emph{exact} positive dual
$Q_L=sF_LF_L^T\succeq0$, where $s>0$ is the rational scale in the
metadata (here $s=1$). Thus positivity of these duals does not depend on
a numerical eigenvalue tolerance. The remaining saved arrays are real
dyadic AC duals. Let $H_J^{\rm AC}$ denote their ambient contraction
adjoint, built using (4), and define
\[
 (H_J^{\rm PT})_{S,T}
      =\sum_L\kappa_{L,J,S,T}(Q_L)_{S,T},\qquad
 H_J=H_J^{\rm AC}-H_J^{\rm PT},\qquad G_J=E_J^TH_JE_J.
\]
Pairing (3) with the AC duals and using (6) yields the exact identity
\[
 0=\sum_J\Tr(G_JC_J)+\sum_L\Tr(Q_LY_L).              \tag{7}
\]
The verifier proves \emph{every} $G_J$ positive definite. Then (1)
makes the first sum strictly positive, while (5) makes the second
nonnegative, a contradiction. This argument applies to arbitrary
complex pure states: real symmetric positive duals pair nonnegatively
with complex Hermitian positive moments, and the complex equations (3)
still vanish. No reality or discrete symmetry of $|\psi\rangle$ is
assumed.

\paragraph{Validated physical representation.}
Every finite floating number in a saved archive is read as its exact
binary rational value. Let $A_{r,J}$ be a stored CFP approximation.
The verifier constructs nearby \emph{exact physical} CFPs recursively,
starting with exact symmetric-pair CFP signs. At level $r$, in
$V\otimes\Sym^{r-1}(V)$, the exact orthogonal projector onto
$\Sym^r(V)$ is
\[
 P_r=\frac{I+(r-1)\Pi\operatorname{Swap}_{12}\Pi}{r},             \tag{8}
\]
where $\Pi$ is projection onto this first-spin/parent space.
Its coupled entries are Racah recouplings of the exact previous CFPs.
Each source spin and multiplicity is independently checked from the
symmetric-power character. Hence its range has exactly the number of
columns supplied in every sector.

Suppose the previous exact CFP family lies within operator norm
$\delta$ of the stored family, block by block. The resulting projector
perturbation is at most
$\beta=(r-1)(2\delta+\delta^2)/r$: recoupling and swap are unitary, and
$\|E^TUE-A^TUA\|_2\le2\delta+\delta^2$.
Arb bounds the residual of the stored projector and the Gram residual
$\zeta=\|A^TA-I\|_F$. Including the projector perturbation gives a bound
$\varepsilon\ge\|(I-P_r)A\|_2$.
For $W=P_rA$,
\[
 \|W^TW-I\|_2\le\zeta+\varepsilon^2=\eta<1.
\]
Projection followed by polar normalization therefore produces an exact
range isometry $E=W(W^TW)^{-1/2}$, with
\[
 \|E-A\|_2\le
 \varepsilon+\frac{\eta}{1+\sqrt{1-\eta}}.                        \tag{9}
\]
Full column rank and the verified multiplicity make it a complete
physical basis, rather than a merely abstract approximate embedding.
The recursion gives the certified sixth-copy distance $\delta_6$.

The ambient AC and partial-time-reversal formulas use the \emph{same}
recursively chosen exact parent family. They contain no additional
approximate parent isometry: its discrepancy has already entered the
recursive projector bounds. Therefore no second parent-distance term
is omitted when the final source CFPs are perturbed.

\paragraph{Validated strict positivity.}
For each source sector, the verifier rebuilds $H_J$ in Arb, forms
$\widetilde G_J=A_{6,J}^TH_JA_{6,J}$, and reads a dyadic congruence
matrix $Z_J$. It checks
\[
 \underbrace{\|I-Z_J\widetilde G_JZ_J^T\|_F}_{\text{interval bound}}
 +\|Z_J\|_F^2(2\delta_6+\delta_6^2)\|H_J\|_F<1.                \tag{10}
\]
The second term bounds the change from $A_{6,J}$ to the exact $E_J$.
Thus $\|I-Z_JG_JZ_J^T\|_2<1$, which proves $Z_JG_JZ_J^T\succ0$.
This also implies $Z_J$ is nonsingular, and hence $G_J\succ0$.
All bounds in (8)--(10), angular coefficients, and matrix products are
outward-rounded Arb enclosures. No optimizer stopping condition,
numerical eigenvalue, or numerical infeasibility declaration is used.
The independent report records all 81 checks, rational bounds and input
hashes.

\paragraph{Exact attainment of order 5.}
Using normalized Dicke states $|D_{27}^{(r)}\rangle$, put
\[
 w=\frac{52-i\sqrt{3433}}{19\sqrt{17}},\qquad
 |\psi_5\rangle=\frac{1}{50}\left[
 \sqrt{327}\bigl(|D_{27}^{(1)}\rangle-|D_{27}^{(26)}\rangle\bigr)
 -i\sqrt{494}\bigl(|D_{27}^{(6)}\rangle-|D_{27}^{(21)}\rangle\bigr)
 -iw\sqrt{429}\bigl(|D_{27}^{(11)}\rangle-|D_{27}^{(16)}\rangle\bigr)
 \right].
\]
Here $|w|=1$ and $2(327+494+429)=2500$. The separate radical-witness
verifier in the catalogue's \texttt{research/n27-n30} folder checks
exactly that $\rho_5=I_6/6$ and
$\langle D_6^{(0)}|\rho_6|D_6^{(0)}\rangle-1/7=17/1593900\ne0$.
Together with the exclusion this establishes maximum order 5.
