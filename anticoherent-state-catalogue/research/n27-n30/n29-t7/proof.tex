\title{No pure order-7 anticoherent state on 29 symmetric qubits}
\author{A validated four-copy separation certificate}\date{November 2026}\maketitle
\begin{theorem}No pure order-7 anticoherent state exists in $V=\operatorname{Sym}^{29}(\mathbb C^2)$. Hence the maximum pure-state AC order is at most 6.\end{theorem}
For a hypothetical normalized state, form the rotation average $X$ of $(|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|)^{\otimes4}$. This is positive, has trace one, and is supported on $\operatorname{Sym}^4(V)$. In doubled total-spin labels, write its physical multiplicity block as $I_{J+1}\otimes B_J/(J+1)$ and put $C_J=B_J/\sqrt{J+1}$. Thus $C_J\succeq0$ and $\sum_J\sqrt{J+1}\Tr C_J=1$. Equivalently, if the unscaled physical block is $B_J^{\rm phys}$, then $C_J=\sqrt{J+1}B_J^{\rm phys}$.

Let $f_\ell(i)$ be the Frobenius-normalized zero component of the rank-$\ell$ tensor on $V$. It is obtained by exact Gram--Schmidt from $(2i-29)^\ell$. Anticoherence imposes $\Tr_1[(\operatorname{diag}f_\ell\otimes I)X]=0$ for $1\le\ell\le 7$. Let $E_{J,S}$ denote normalized coefficients of fractional parentage into $V\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^3(V)$, with three-copy doubled spin $S$. Rotation covariance reduces the contractions to
\[
 R_{\ell,S,T}(C)=\sum_Jc_{\ell,J,S,T}E_{J,S}C_JE_{J,T}^T=0.
\]
For $S\le T$, the verifier evaluates the coefficient directly:
\[
 c_{\ell,J,S,T}=\frac{\sum_{i=0}^{29}f_\ell(i)
 \langle j,m_i;S/2,S/2|J/2,S/2+m_i\rangle
 \langle j,m_i;T/2,S/2|J/2,S/2+m_i\rangle}
 {\sqrt{J+1}\,|\langle S/2,S/2;T/2,-S/2|\ell,0\rangle|},
 \quad j=\frac{29}{2},\quad m_i=i-j.
\]
The denominator fixes the output tensor to be positive at its upper common weight. Forbidden components are zero. Direct exact-factorial CG sums remove any reliance on a source-spin-dependent sign convention. The corresponding compact expression is
\[
 c_{\ell,J,S,T}=(-1)^{(29+T+J)/2}\sqrt{J+1}
 \begin{Bmatrix}j&j&\ell\\T/2&S/2&J/2\end{Bmatrix}.
\]

The dyadic matrices $D_{\ell,S,T}$ pair with the contraction equations. In addition, $X$ is separable across the split $2|2$, hence its partial transpose, followed by first-pair time reversal, is positive. Denote its normalized invariant output blocks by $Y_L\succeq0$, with the same Hilbert--Schmidt convention $Y_L=B^{\rm PT}_L/\sqrt{L+1}$. The dual output matrices are encoded as exact dyadic Gram factors $Q_L=M_LM_L^T\succeq0$.

Let $(P,Q)$ label canonical first-pair spin and second-pair spin in the certified two-copy gauge. Symmetric-pair doubled spins are $58,54,\ldots,2$. In pair-pair coordinates the partial-time-reversal coefficient is
\[
 \kappa_{L,J;P,Q,R,S}=\sqrt{(J+1)(L+1)}
 \begin{Bmatrix}P/2&Q/2&J/2\\R/2&S/2&L/2\end{Bmatrix}.
\]
It maps the source entry $((P,Q),(R,S))$ to the output entry $((R,Q),(P,S))$. This is the Racah recoupling identity for the four real CG factors and first-pair time-reversal phases; the symmetric-pair parity gives the displayed positive overall sign. Second-pair gauge signs occur on both sides and cancel. The verifier recomputes these exact Racah coefficients with enclosures. Thus define
\[
 (K_J)_{(P,Q),(R,S)}=\sum_L\kappa_{L,J;P,Q,R,S}
 (Q_L)_{(R,Q),(P,S)}.
\]
Forbidden couplings are zero.

The recoupling map $T_J$ from $V\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^3(V)$ into the symmetric pair-pair channels has entries
\[
 (T_J)_{(P,Q),(S,a)}=(-1)^{29+(Q+J)/2}\sqrt{(S+1)(P+1)}
 \begin{Bmatrix}29/2&29/2&P/2\\Q/2&J/2&S/2\end{Bmatrix}
 (E^{(3)}_S)_{Q,a}.
\]
It is a unitary Racah recoupling followed by an orthogonal channel projection, so $\|T_J\|_2\le1$. Let $(H_J^{\rm AC})_{S,T}=\sum_\ell c_{\ell,J,S,T}D_{\ell,S,T}$, with transpose blocks below the diagonal, and set
\[
 G_J=E_J^T[H_J^{\rm AC}-T_J^TK_JT_J]E_J.
\]
Pairing all necessary conditions gives
\[
 0=\sum_J\Tr(G_JC_J)+\sum_L\Tr(Q_LY_L).
\]
The verifier proves $G_J\succ0$ in every source sector. Both terms on the right are nonnegative, so all $C_J$ must vanish, contradicting trace one. The real symmetric matrices also cover arbitrary complex Hermitian source moments.

\paragraph{Validated representation and positivity.}
All stored floating arrays encode exact binary rationals. Starting with exact symmetric-pair CFP signs, the verifier constructs exact nearby CFPs recursively. The exact symmetric projector at level $r$ is $(I+(r-1)\Pi\operatorname{Swap}_{12}\Pi)/r$. Its coupled-basis entries are exact Racah coefficients. Every sector and multiplicity is independently checked against the symmetric-power character. If previous CFPs are within operator norm $\delta$ of their dyadic approximants, the projector perturbation is at most $(r-1)(2\delta+\delta^2)/r$.

Arb enclosures bound the projector residual $\varepsilon$ and Gram residual $\zeta$. Projection onto the exact range and polar normalization give an exact isometry $E$ with
\[
 \|E-A\|_2\le\varepsilon+\frac{\eta}{1+\sqrt{1-\eta}},\qquad\eta=\zeta+\varepsilon^2<1.
\]
Let $\delta_3,\delta_4$ be the resulting third- and fourth-copy bounds. The point recoupling $\widetilde T_J$ formed from dyadic third-copy CFPs obeys $\|T_J-\widetilde T_J\|_2\le\delta_3$. Put $\widetilde H_J=H_J^{\rm AC}-\widetilde T_J^TK_J\widetilde T_J$. The exact source Gram differs from $A_J^T\widetilde H_JA_J$ by at most
\[
 e_J=(2\delta_4+\delta_4^2)\|\widetilde H_J\|_F+
 (2\delta_3+\delta_3^2)\|K_J\|_F.
\]
For every source block the verifier checks, with outward-rounded balls,
\[
 \|I-Z_J(A_J^T\widetilde H_JA_J)Z_J^T\|_F+\|Z_J\|_F^2e_J<1.
\]
Here $Z_J$ is an exact dyadic approximate inverse Cholesky factor. This proves positive definiteness of $G_J$ without trusting any numerical eigensolver or optimizer.
