\title{Twenty-eight qubits: exclusion of eighth-order pure anticoherence}
\author{Exact three-copy certificate}\date{5 November 2026}\maketitle
\begin{theorem}
No pure symmetric twenty-eight-qubit state is eighth-order anticoherent.
Consequently $K(28,t)=0$ for every $t\ge8$, and the maximum pure AC order is at most seven.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $V=V_{14}$. A hypothetical normalized eighth-order AC vector $\psi\in V$ has a rotation-averaged three-copy moment
\[
X=\int_{SU(2)}(U_g|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|U_g^\dagger)^{\otimes3}\,dg
\]
on $W=\operatorname{Sym}^3(V)$, of dimension $\binom{31}{3}=4495$.
It is positive, invariant, and has trace one. For each spin-tensor rank $1\le\ell\le8$, anticoherence implies the necessary first-leg contraction equations
\[
\Tr_1[(T_{\ell0}\otimes I^{\otimes2})X]=0. \tag{1}
\]
The certificate supplies a complete physical decomposition
\[
W=\bigoplus_J V_J\otimes\mathbb C^{m_J},\qquad
\sum_J(2J+1)m_J=4495,
\]
with 41 nonempty sectors, $\sum_Jm_J=113$, and 249 real symmetric invariant coordinates. Integer highest-weight columns $H_J$ are given in the binomial basis $b_i=\sqrt{\binom{28}{i}}|D_{28}^{(i)}\rangle$ and averaged symmetric words $e_{abc}$. Their squared norms are $\prod_i\binom{28}{i}^{o_i}o_i!/3!$, with $o_i$ the word occupancies. Their descendants $U_{J,s}=J_-^sH_J/s!$ obey
\[
U_{J,s}^\dagger U_{J,s}=\binom{2J}{s}H_J^\dagger H_J.
\]
Thus every real invariant operator has unique block coordinates
\[
X(B)=\sum_Jd_J\sum_{s=0}^{2J}\frac{U_{J,s}B_JU_{J,s}^T}{\binom{2J}{s}},\qquad B_J=B_J^T,
\]
where $d_J>0$ are the saved denominator scales. Positivity is equivalent to $B_J\succeq0$ in every sector. Taking real parts of complex Hermitian multiplicity matrices preserves positivity, the real contraction equations, and trace; hence this reduction covers arbitrary complex $\psi$.

The diagonal tensors are represented by the degree-$\ell$ polynomial in $2i-28$ orthogonal to all lower degrees under uniform summation over $i=0,\ldots,28$. The certificate selects 212 physical matrix entries of (1), with primitive integer rows $R_a(B)$. Every hypothetical moment satisfies those rows, regardless of whether they form a complete constraint set.

For supplied positive dyadic diagonal matrices $S_J$, put
\[
x_J=d_J(2J+1)S_JB_JS_J\succeq0.
\]
The exact rational certificate consists of rational row weights $y_a$ and rational symmetric matrices $C_J$ satisfying coefficientwise
\[
\boxed{-\Tr X=\sum_{a=1}^{212}y_aR_a(B)+\sum_J\Tr(C_Jx_J).} \tag{2}
\]
All 41 matrices $C_J$ are positive definite: their 113 rational LDL pivots are strictly positive, and their exact reconstructions are checked. Substituting (1) and $\Tr X=1$ into (2) yields $-1\ge0$, a contradiction. Higher orders are excluded by nesting. No numerical infeasibility, constellation symmetry, or partial-transpose assumption is used in the proof.
\end{proof}
