Write $D_r=|D_N^{(r)}\rangle$ for the normalized Dicke basis.
The following normalized states have strictly the indicated AC orders:
\begin{align*}
|\psi_{21}\rangle&=\frac{\sqrt{915}(D_0+D_{21})+
 13\sqrt{14}(D_4-D_{17})+17\sqrt7(D_8+D_{13})}{4\sqrt{663}},\\
|\psi_{22}\rangle&=\frac{3\sqrt{11}(D_1+D_{21})+
 \sqrt{154}(D_6-D_{16})+\sqrt{119}D_{11}}{25}.
\end{align*}

\paragraph{Diagonal moments.}
The Dicke-index probability distributions are invariant under $r\mapsto N-r$,
so every odd moment of $m=r-N/2$ vanishes. At $N=21$, direct rational arithmetic
gives $\langle m^2\rangle=161/4$ and
$\langle m^4\rangle=46529/16$. At $N=22$, it gives
$\langle m^2\rangle=44$ and $\langle m^4\rangle=3476$.
These are the moments of the uniform spin distributions at the corresponding
spins: in general they equal $j(j+1)/3$ and
$j(j+1)(3j^2+3j-1)/15$, respectively. Together with normalization and the odd
moments, this matches all diagonal moments through orders four and five.
The diagonal entries of the $q$-body reduction are expectations of
hypergeometric probabilities, which are polynomials of degree $q$ in $r$.
Matching these moments therefore gives every diagonal entry $1/(q+1)$.

\paragraph{Off-diagonal cancellation.}
For amplitudes $c_r$ the general reduced-state entry is
\[
(\rho_q)_{ab}=\sum_l c_{a+l}\overline{c_{b+l}}
 \binom{N-q}{l}
 \sqrt{\frac{\binom qa\binom qb}
 {\binom N{a+l}\binom N{b+l}}}.
\]
At $N=21$, support differences one, two and three are absent.
At order four the only potentially nonzero off-diagonal entries are $(0,4)$
and its conjugate. The contributions from pairs $(0,4)$ and $(17,21)$ cancel,
as do those from $(4,8)$ and $(13,17)$. Reflection makes their binomial
prefactors equal and the sign at index 17 makes them opposite.
Thus $\rho_4=I_5/5$.
At $N=22$, support differences one through four are absent. At order five,
the pairs $(1,6)$ and $(16,21)$ cancel, as do $(6,11)$ and $(11,16)$.
Thus $\rho_5=I_6/6$.

\paragraph{Strict order.}
The next reductions have the exact defects
\[
(\rho_5^{\psi_{21}})_{0,5}=\frac{11}{912}\ne0,
\qquad
(\rho_6^{\psi_{22}})_{0,0}-\frac17=-\frac1{33915}\ne0.
\]
Hence these states are not fifth- and sixth-order AC, respectively.
These observations establish exact attainment by particular states, without
asserting that their AC orders are globally maximal.
