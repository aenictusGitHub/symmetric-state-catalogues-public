Universal upper bounds

The exclusions apply to every complex symmetric pure state, without fixing its support or symmetry. For a hypothetical state, average three copies of its projector for the 21-qubit proof, or four copies for the 22-qubit proof, over collective rotations to form a positive operator X of trace one. Anticoherence makes its low-multipole contractions vanish. In invariant block coordinates an exact rational identity has the form

-\operatorname{Tr}X=\sum_i y_i R_i(X)+\sum_J\operatorname{Tr}(C_Jx_J)+\sum_L\operatorname{Tr}(Q_LY_L)

Here R_i(X)=0, x_J\succeq0 and every certificate matrix is positive definite. The last sum is needed only for the 21-qubit proof: Y_L are positive compressions of the partial time reversal of X. Positivity follows because the rotational average of tensor-power projectors is separable across the first copy and the other two. Thus the right side is nonnegative while the left side is −1, a contradiction.

What is not proved

Exact attainment by these particular states does not establish that their orders are globally maximal. The remaining question is fifth-order existence at N=21. The tested three-copy and four-copy positivity relaxations did not exclude it; this is not an existence result for pure states. The catalogue keeps this question unresolved. The finite searches and relaxation tests are documented in the research report. An additional independently verified three-copy seventh-order exclusion at N=22 is retained as an auxiliary certificate; the stronger sixth-order exclusion proves the maximum.
