A\mathbf b=0,\qquad A\in\mathbb Z^{27\times27},

\[
\det A\ne0\ \Longrightarrow\ \mathbf b=0\ \Longrightarrow\ X=0,
\]
contradicting \(\operatorname{Tr}X=1\). Thus no pure symmetric eleven-qubit state is 4-anticoherent.
