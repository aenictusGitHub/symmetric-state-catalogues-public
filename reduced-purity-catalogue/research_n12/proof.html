The twelve-qubit half-system purity minimum

For every normalized pure symmetric twelve-qubit state, $P_6=\operatorname{tr}(\rho_6^2)\ge13/84$. The icosahedral state attains equality.

Quartic spin-covariant certificate

Write $\rho=|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$ on the spin-6 space. Let $a_\ell=\sum_{m=-\ell}^\ell|\operatorname{tr}(T_{\ell m}^\dagger\rho)|^2$, where the irreducible spin tensors are orthonormal for the Hilbert–Schmidt inner product. Verification of the identity

For the spin $s/2$ reduction, use the same Hilbert–Schmidt normalization for the tensors. The adjoint of partial trace is an intertwiner, so it acts by a scalar on each rank $\ell\le s$. To obtain the scalar, use the highest-weight operator $J_+^\ell$. Its expectation in a coherent product of $m$ qubits scales as $m!/(m-\ell)!$ at fixed direction. Its squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm is $(\ell!)^2\binom{m+\ell+1}{2\ell+1}$, from summing the squared raising matrix elements in the Dicke basis. The swap trick expresses $P_s$ as the expectation of the swap of $s$ corresponding qubits across two copies. Compress the swap to the tensor product of the two symmetric spaces. It commutes with collective rotations, and the tensor product of two spin-6 spaces is multiplicity-free. The compressed swap therefore has one scalar $M_{sJ}$ per total spin. Only even $J$ occur in $\psi\otimes\psi$, because this vector is symmetric under interchange of the full copies. Thus $P_s=\sum_{J\text{ even}}M_{sJ}p_J$ and $\sum_{J\text{ even}}p_J=1$.
