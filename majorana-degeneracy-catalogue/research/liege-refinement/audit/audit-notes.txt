Independent audit of the reported-order Liège refinements
========================================================

Scope and interpretation
------------------------
The primary audit covers all 144 available imported coordinate-state records,
at each record's original reported order. It makes no higher-order search and
does not interpret the reported order as a proven maximum. The 16 unmatched
original Dicke-file alternatives are checked separately and receive no inferred
Majorana partition.

The high-precision coefficients are numerical vectors. A residual below 1e-60
does not, by itself, prove that an exact anticoherent state exists nearby. Nor
does 85-digit serialization assert 85 independently certified digits of a unique
exact solution: phase, rotations, and other solution freedoms remain. The audit
verifies the equations actually satisfied by the serialized numerical vector.

Algorithm review
----------------
The refiner forms the real residual of rho_t-I/(t+1). Off-diagonal real and
imaginary parts are weighted by sqrt(2), making its Euclidean norm exactly the
matrix Hilbert-Schmidt/Frobenius norm. For c_r=x_r+i y_r, the off-diagonal term
c_r conjugate(c_s) has real part x_r x_s+y_r y_s and imaginary part
y_r x_s-x_r y_s. The analytic Jacobian in refine.py has the corresponding
derivatives, including the correct imaginary signs.

A double-precision SVD gives only an approximate inverse. Repeated corrections
can nonetheless improve a high-precision residual far below double precision:
the inaccurate inverse changes the convergence rate, not the residual's
evaluation precision. Rank truncation or stagnation could prevent convergence,
so the final test must recompute every equation from the serialized coefficients.
The refiner does this, and this audit independently repeats the calculation at
110 digits without importing any refiner functions.

Five singular cases use a justified separate construction, not an ordinary
minimum-norm Newton correction. With n=3g+1, order2, and a g-fold south pole,
moment saturation forces support {g,n}. The probabilities are

  |c_g|^2 = n/(4g+2),   |c_n|^2 = (g+1)/(4g+2).

Their gap is 2g+1>2, so all off-diagonal entries of rho_2 vanish. The first two
binomial moments give its three equal diagonal entries. Each retained component
keeps its original phase, giving the nearest point in this forced two-term family
for the fixed amplitudes. These five IDs are acs.7.2.2, acs.10.2.3, acs.13.2.4,
acs.16.2.5, and acs.19.2.6. Their larger corrections are explicitly recorded.

Independent reduced-state check
------------------------------
Define z_r=c_r/sqrt(binomial(n,r)). The verifier evaluates directly

  (rho_t)_(a,b) = sqrt(binomial(t,a) binomial(t,b))
                 sum_(k=0)^(n-t) binomial(n-t,k) z_(a+k) conjugate(z_(b+k)).

It checks normalization separately, evaluates all matrix entries, and records
the Frobenius residual before and after refinement. Original high-precision
vectors must agree, up to phase, with the imported 35-digit vectors within1e-33.
Every output coefficient is read from its decimal string. Component changes are
reported both before and after global-phase alignment.

Pole roots and the full partition
--------------------------------
The polynomial convention is P(z)=sum_r sqrt(binomial(n,r)) c_r z^r, arising
from the product of the source physical spinors (a_i+b_i z). Its multiplicities
equal those of the physical constellation. Exact leading zero coefficients
represent a south-pole cluster; exact trailing zeros represent a north-pole
cluster. Every repeated source pole is retained as literal decimal zeros.
The first and last remaining coefficients are nonzero, so those multiplicities
are exactly the specified values, rather than mere lower bounds.

All remaining roots are checked against the original points, using the chart
z=-a/b where |z|<=1 and the reciprocal chart w=-b/a otherwise. Centres are rounded
to rational decimal values; interval arithmetic bounds the rounding difference
from the original source point. After factoring the exact repeated-pole zeros,
interval Horner translation gives enclosures B_k for

  P(c+w) = sum_k B_k w^k.

For every remaining source point, the verifier finds a rational radius R with

  upper(|B_0| + sum_(k>=2)|B_k| R^k) < lower(|B_1| R).

Rouche's theorem then gives exactly one root, counting multiplicity, in that
disk. Inverse stereographic projection maps the disk inside a spherical cap
of chord radius2R around its rational centre. The verifier also checks, with
outward interval bounds, that every pair of centre caps is disjoint, including
caps expressed in opposite charts and the structural poles. The number of
disks equals the degree after removing the repeated-pole factors. Consequently
all those remaining roots are simple, and the serialized numerical polynomial
has the same full partition as the source record. This is a root statement
about the serialized vector, not an exact anticoherence certificate.

Implementation and portability
------------------------------
verify_refinement.py requires only Python's standard library and mpmath.
verify_alternates.py imports the independent reduction and change routines from
verify_refinement.py. No source importer or refiner module is imported. Inputs
are supplied explicitly, and their SHA256 hashes are stored in the reports.

Example commands, from an exported package containing these audit scripts:

  python3 verify_refinement.py --original observations.json \
    --refined refined.json --out verification.json

  python3 verify_alternates.py --original alternate-inputs.json \
    --refined alternate-refined.json --out alternate-verification.json

The interval root checks use mpmath.iv at110 decimal digits and directed rounding.
Printed interval endpoints and ratios are also rounded outward in decimal, using
the exact binary endpoint numerator and denominator rather than nearest rounding.
The inequalities have large margins; they do not rely on floating comparisons
close to equality. Verification results and per-root disk data are retained in
verification.json, rather than only a success flag.

Results of the complete audit
-----------------------------
All144 primary records pass. The worst reported-order Hilbert-Schmidt residual
decreased from9.06346774220875e-14 to5.87109350591239e-66. The largest final norm
error is1.05156276677980e-65. The largest phase-aligned coefficient change is
1.13580250313732e-7, in the singular two-term family correction. The frozen
originals agree with the imported35-digit vectors to within6.545e-36.

The root audit covers5,437 disjoint simple-root disks and238 structural pole
roots, for5,675 total Majorana points. The smallest relative Rouche margin is
greater than0.751; the smallest cap-separation ratio is greater than109,341.
Every full source partition is preserved by the serialized numerical vector.
The conservative maximum source-to-root spherical displacement bound is below
2.01e-6 (approximately2e-6).

All16 download-only Dicke alternates pass, with worst residual1.33308543768092e-70
and maximum norm error1.97542612203068e-71. Their partitions remain unassigned.

The separately generated75-digit sphere coordinates also pass an independent
physical-spinor-product reconstruction in verify_points.py. All144 point sets
reproduce their refined coefficients up to global phase with maximum component
error1.53792098806346e-66. Their greatest point displacement from the originals
is1.80121559924813e-7; their maximum unit-radius error is7.666e-76. This consistency
test is numerical; the independent interval disks establish multiplicities.

  python3 verify_points.py --refined refined.json \
    --points refined-points.json --out points-verification.json
