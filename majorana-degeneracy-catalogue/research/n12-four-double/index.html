A rigorous obstruction when the four doubled points form antipodal pairs

The result proved here is restricted: a pure symmetric 12-qubit state of full Majorana partition (2,2,2,2,1,1,1,1) cannot be 3-anticoherent if its four doubled points form two antipodal pairs. The four simple points are unrestricted. This does not settle the partition for arbitrary doubled-point geometry.

The proof uses exact rational polynomial identities and real-root counts. Run python verify-antipodal-obstruction.py from this directory (SymPy is required). The output is antipodal-obstruction-verification.json. The verifier reconstructs its matrices and equations rather than importing the discovery scripts.

1. Complete normal form for the stated family

Two distinct unoriented antipodal axes lie in a plane. A spatial rotation sends that plane to the equator. A further rotation about the polar axis places the four points at \pm \exp(i\theta ), \pm \exp(-i\theta ) in the root coordinate. Distinctness gives 0<\theta <\pi /2. Consequently the double-root factor can be written

Q(z)=z^4+2hz^2+1,\qquad h=-\cos(2\theta)\in(-1,1).

Every state in the stated family therefore has Majorana polynomial P=Q^2R, with an arbitrary complex quartic R. A degree below four is allowed; the missing degree records roots at infinity. Common roots of Q and R, repeated roots of R, and the endpoints h=\pm 1 cannot occur in the target full partition. We permit these degenerations during the algebraic calculation and exclude them when identifying the surviving partition.

We use the normalized-Dicke convention

P(z)=\sum_{r=0}^{12}(-1)^r\sqrt{\binom{12}{r}}c_rz^r

up to a nonzero scalar. Rotations and global phase do not affect anticoherence or multiplicities.

2. Third-order anticoherence forces the residual quartic to be antipodal

Consider the time-reversal-real quartic basis

B=(z^4+1,\ z^2,\ i(z^4-1),\ z^3-z,\ i(z^3+z)).

It is a complex basis of all quartics, including polynomials of lower degree. Write R=\sum (x_j+i y_j)B_j, with real vectors x,y. Since Q is time-reversal real, the corresponding spin-six vectors W_j=Q^2B_j are also time-reversal real. The odd part of the pure density operator is therefore a linear combination of

i(W_iW_j^\dagger-W_jW_i^\dagger),\quad i<j,

with coefficients given, up to a common sign, by x_i y_j-y_i x_j.

Taking a three-qubit reduced state commutes with time reversal. The identity \rho _3=I_4/4 is time-reversal even, so the reduced image of this odd part must vanish. The verifier constructs all 16 real components of this reduction as a 16\times 10 matrix M(h). Explicitly, before positive row scalings, the matrix entries follow from

\rho_{ac}=(-1)^{a+c}\sqrt{\binom3a\binom3c} \sum_{q=0}^9\frac{\binom9q p_{a+q}\overline{p_{c+q}}} {\binom{12}{a+q}\binom{12}{c+q}}.

The selected ten rows listed in odd-restriction.json have determinant

-697827328000000(h^2+3)^2 A_0(h)A_-(h)A_+(h),

where the three degree-twelve factors are recorded in that file and in the verification output. The verifier recomputes the determinant and uses exact Sturm counts to establish that none of its factors has a real root in [-1,1]. Thus M has rank ten throughout the allowed interval. All wedge coordinates x_i y_j-y_i x_j vanish, so x,y are proportional. A global phase makes R time-reversal real.

This step does not assume that the four simple points were antipodal. It proves that they would have to be antipodal in any candidate 3-AC state in this family.

3. Exact elimination in the time-reversal-real family

After the phase choice, put

R=(b+id)z^4+(e+if)z^3+cz^2+(-e+if)z+b-id, \qquad b,c,d,e,f\in\mathbb R.

Odd moments vanish automatically. Second-order isotropy is equivalent to the five real equations from \langle J_z^2\rangle =14, \langle \{J_z,J_+\}\rangle =0, and \langle J_+^2\rangle =0. Their primitive integer polynomials are in antipodal-reduction.json; the verifier independently reconstructs them from the Dicke coefficients.

  • If ef\ne 0, set t=f/e and solve the two linear off-diagonal equations for b,c in terms of d. The remaining off-diagonal equation is
t^2D(h)K(h)e^2+16(h^2+1) [t^2A(h)+A(-h)]d^2=0,

where

D=(h^2-2h+5)(h^2+2h+5),\quad K=7h^4+18h^2+7,
A=h^6+2h^5+9h^4+12h^3+51h^2+34h+115.

Here D,K>0 and A(\pm h)\ge 67 on [-1,1]. Since e\ne 0, this is impossible. The derivation does not divide by d, so it includes d=0.

  • If exactly one of e,f is nonzero, another off-diagonal equation forces d=0. For example, when e=0, its coefficient is h^4+4h^3+12h^2+16h+23\ge 3. The remaining linear equation fixes c=2b(2h^2-h+9)/(h^2-h+2). The two diagonal conditions then form a homogeneous linear system in b^2,f^2. Its determinant is
48(h-3)(h+1)^3(h^2+3)(h^2-h+2)^2 (h^3-4h^2+h-2)(h^3+h^2-h-17).

It has no root in (-1,1), verified exactly. The f=0 branch is also checked directly. Both branches contradict the nonzero active component.

  • If e=f=0 but d\ne 0, the last equation fixes c=-2bh/(h^2+2). The diagonal equation, after multiplying by its positive denominator, becomes
(-16h^8+1104h^4+4320h^2+4320)b^2 +(-16h^8-48h^6+1088h^4+4416h^2+4352)d^2=0.

The coefficients are at least 4304 and 4288 on [-1,1], respectively. This is impossible.

  • In the remaining branch d=e=f=0, suppose first that b\ne 0 and rescale it to one. Eliminating c gives
3072h^2(h-3)^2(h+3)^2(h^2+3)^2 (8h^6+51h^4+258h^2+195)=0.

The only root in (-1,1) is h=0, where the first equation is 1080=0. Hence b=0. The nonzero-state condition now gives c\ne 0; the diagonal equation is -2c^2h^2(3h^2+11)=0, forcing h=0.

4. Multiplicity guard and conclusion

The sole surviving projective polynomial is

P(z)=z^2(z^4+1)^2.

It has four simple roots of z^4+1, each doubled, a double root at zero, and a double root at infinity because its affine degree is ten while n=12. Its full partition is therefore (2,2,2,2,2,2), the double octahedron. The verifier checks both this square-free decomposition and \rho _3=I_4/4 exactly.

The survivor lies outside (2^4,1^4). Therefore any hypothetical 3-AC state in the target partition must have a doubled-point configuration that is not two antipodal pairs. No global nonexistence claim follows from this restriction.

An exact sequence explains the near-zero residuals

Every positive parameter gives exactly four double and four simple roots: the quartic discriminants and their resultant are nonzero. As the parameter tends to zero, the normalized state approaches a third-order state with six double points. Thus the infimum of the third-order defect within the target partition is zero. This proves neither attainment nor nonattainment.
