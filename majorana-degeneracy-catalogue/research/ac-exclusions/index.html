How the reports were assessed

The 2020 paper states the two absence claims in Sections 4.4.3 and 4.4.4, without giving their exclusion proofs there. The 2015 primary table explicitly describes its maximal orders as conjectured; the companion paper specifically calls the nine-qubit third-order exclusion a conjecture. Numerical searches and spherical-design bounds alone do not supply the required proof.

All certificates above are independently derived for this catalogue. The eleven-qubit fourth-order case was previously unresolved here; its absence in the 2017 numerical survey was not used as proof. The standard equivalence between anticoherence and vanishing low-rank spin multipoles supplies the link from the identity to the theorem.

  • Quantum 4, 285 (2020), PDF p.11 — the original reported exclusions.
