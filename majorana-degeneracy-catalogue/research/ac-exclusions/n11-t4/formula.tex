\paragraph{Theorem.} No nonzero vector in $\operatorname{Sym}^{11}(\mathbb C^2)$ is fourth-order anticoherent. The result allows arbitrary complex Dicke amplitudes and assumes no discrete symmetry, sparse support, or reality condition.

Let $T_{\ell q}$ be an irreducible spherical-tensor family for spin $11/2$, in the usual Wigner convention, with $0\le\ell\le11$ and $-\ell\le q\le\ell$. Define
\[
 I_{\ell m k}(\psi)=\sum_{q+r+s=0}
 \begin{pmatrix}\ell&m&k\\q&r&s\end{pmatrix}
 \langle\psi|T_{\ell q}|\psi\rangle
 \langle\psi|T_{mr}|\psi\rangle
 \langle\psi|T_{ks}|\psi\rangle,
 \qquad
 W_{\ell m k}(\psi)=\frac{I_{\ell m k}(\psi)}{I_{\ell m k}(D_{11}^{(0)})}.
\]
The parentheses are Wigner $3j$ symbols. Every triple below satisfies the triangle inequalities and has even $\ell+m+k$. Consequently its zero-projection $3j$ symbol is nonzero. The coherent tensor moments of rank at most $11$ are also nonzero, so all displayed denominators are nonzero. Any nonzero rescaling of an entire tensor-rank family cancels in $W$.

\paragraph{Exact identity.} For every complex, not necessarily normalized, spin-$11/2$ vector,
\[
 \boxed{\sum_{\ell m k}b_{\ell m k}W_{\ell m k}(\psi)=\langle\psi|\psi\rangle^3.}
\]

\paragraph{Contradiction.} Every listed triple contains a rank in $\{1,2,3,4\}$. Fourth-order anticoherence sets all tensor expectations of those ranks to zero. Every invariant on the left therefore vanishes, whereas the right is strictly positive for a nonzero vector. This proves the theorem. Together with an exact third-order construction, it proves that the maximal anticoherence order for eleven qubits is three.

\paragraph{Exact rational verification.} For completeness, the verifier's convention is specified here. Put
\[
 e_r=\sqrt{\binom{11}{r}}\,|D_{11}^{(r)}\rangle,
 \qquad |\psi\rangle=\sum_{r=0}^{11}z_r e_r,
 \qquad G_{rr}=\binom{11}{r}.
\]
Choose $J_z|D_{11}^{(r)}\rangle=(r-11/2)|D_{11}^{(r)}\rangle$, reversing the source spin-axis convention $m=11/2-r$. Anticoherence and the scalar contractions are rotation-invariant. In this convention,
\[
 J_+e_r=(r+1)e_{r+1},\qquad J_-e_r=(12-r)e_{r-1}.
\]
Terms outside $0\le r\le11$ are zero. Construct integer matrices
\[
 F_{\ell q}=\frac{(\operatorname{ad}J_-)^{\ell-q}(J_+)^{\ell}}{(\ell-q)!\,\ell!},
 \qquad (\operatorname{ad}A)(B)=AB-BA.
\]
They differ from conventional tensors by a rank-dependent nonzero constant and the factor $\sqrt{\binom{2\ell}{\ell-q}}$. Thus the rational scalar contractions use
\[
 h^{qrs}_{\ell m k}=\frac{\begin{pmatrix}\ell&m&k\\q&r&s\end{pmatrix}}
 {\sqrt{\binom{2\ell}{\ell-q}\binom{2m}{m-r}\binom{2k}{k-s}}},
 \qquad g^{qrs}_{\ell m k}=h^{qrs}_{\ell m k}/h_{\rm seed},
\]
where $h_{\rm seed}$ is the first nonzero value in ascending lexicographic $(q,r)$ order, with $s=-q-r$. The ratios $g$ are rational. Let $f_{\ell m k}$ denote the contraction using $F$ and $g$. Then
\[
 f_{\ell m k}(D_{11}^{(0)})=g^{000}_{\ell m k}
 \binom{11}{\ell}\binom{11}{m}\binom{11}{k},
 \qquad W_{\ell m k}=f_{\ell m k}/f_{\ell m k}(D_{11}^{(0)}).
\]
The machine-readable certificate records $\alpha_{\ell m k}=b_{\ell m k}/f_{\ell m k}(D_{11}^{(0)})$ and verifies
\[
 \sum_{\ell m k}\alpha_{\ell m k}f_{\ell m k}(\psi)
 =\left(\sum_{r=0}^{11}\binom{11}{r}|z_r|^2\right)^3.
\]
The independent verifier constructs the matrices directly from the commutators and expands this equality in independent formal variables $z_r$ and $\overline{z_r}$. All $974{,}308$ rational contributions across $5{,}664$ monomial slots cancel exactly. No numerical arithmetic, optimization output, or irreducible-block decomposition is used in that verification.
