\paragraph{Ten-qubit exclusion.}
For an arbitrary pure spin-$5$ state $|\psi\rangle$, let
\[
 I_{\ell m k}(\psi)=\sum_{q+r+s=0}
 \begin{pmatrix}\ell&m&k\\q&r&s\end{pmatrix}
 \langle\psi|T_{\ell q}|\psi\rangle
 \langle\psi|T_{mr}|\psi\rangle
 \langle\psi|T_{ks}|\psi\rangle,
 \qquad
 W_{\ell m k}(\psi)=\frac{I_{\ell m k}(\psi)}{I_{\ell m k}(D_{10}^{(0)})}.
\]
The denominator is nonzero for each even triangle triple below. The following identity holds for every complex $|\psi\rangle$:
\[
 \sum_{\ell m k}b_{\ell m k}W_{\ell m k}(\psi)=\langle\psi|\psi\rangle^3.
\]
\[
\begin{array}{c|r}
(\ell,m,k)&b_{\ell m k}\\\hline
(0,1,1) & \frac{115}{4} \\
(0,2,2) & \frac{225}{4} \\
(0,3,3) & \frac{135}{4} \\
(0,4,4) & \frac{37}{4} \\
(1,1,2) & 60 \\
(1,2,3) & \frac{135}{2} \\
(1,3,4) & \frac{5}{6} \\
(1,4,5) & -\frac{275}{12} \\
(2,2,4) & -\frac{25}{2} \\
(2,3,3) & -\frac{135}{4} \\
(2,3,5) & -\frac{125}{2} \\
(2,4,4) & -\frac{3175}{132} \\
(2,4,6) & -\frac{875}{44} \\
(3,3,4) & -\frac{1495}{44} \\
(3,3,6) & -\frac{625}{44} \\
(3,4,5) & -\frac{425}{26} \\
(3,4,7) & -\frac{6125}{858} \\
(4,4,4) & -\frac{279}{52} \\
(4,4,6) & -\frac{25}{12} \\
(4,4,8) & -\frac{175}{286} \\
\end{array}
\]
Every listed triple contains a rank in $\{1,2,3,4\}$. For a $4$-anticoherent state, every invariant on the left therefore vanishes. The right is positive for a nonzero state, a contradiction. The identity is verified coefficientwise using exact rational arithmetic; it imposes no symmetry or reality restriction on $|\psi\rangle$.
