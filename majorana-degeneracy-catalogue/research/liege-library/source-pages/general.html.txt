							<h2 class="title"><span class="in"><span style="font-style: normal; font-size: 20px; font-family: AvenirNext-Bold; text-shadow: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0) 0.000000000000000006123233849335533px 0.10000000149011612px 4px;">Library of spin anticoherent states</span></span></h2>
							<div class="article">
								<div class="article-content">
									<div class="RichTextElement">
										<div><p><br /><span style="font-size: 14px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular; color: rgb(0, 0, 0);">&#10095;&#10095; </span><span style="font-size: 14px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> <strong><a href="libspinACstatesdeg.html">Library of spin anticoherent states with degeneracy</a></strong></span></p><p><span style="font-size: 14px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular; color: rgb(0, 0, 0);">&#10095;&#10095; </span><span style="font-size: 14px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> <strong><a href="lib3dsphericaldesign.html">Library of 3-d spherical designs with degeneracy</a></strong></span></p><p><span style="font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><br /></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">Anticoherent spin-<em>j</em> states to order </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">t</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> are states<br /></span></p>
											
											<div class="first wide center">
												<div style="" class="graphic">
													<div class="figure-content HTMLElement">
														<span>
														<div style="line-height:1.35em; font-family: Avenir Next; font-size: 13px;">
														
														<body>
														$$|\psi\rangle\in\mathbb{C}^{2j+1}\quad\textrm{such that}\quad\langle\psi|(\mathbf{J}\boldsymbol{\cdot}\mathbf{n})^{k}|\psi\rangle\quad\textrm{is independent of }\mathbf{n}\textrm{ for }k=1,\ldots,t$$
														</body>
														
														</div>
														</span>
													</div>
												</div>
											</div><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">These states are equivalent to symmetric <em>N</em>-qubit states (<em>N=2j</em>) with maximally mixed <em>t</em>-qubit reductions in the symmetric sector; see</span><span style="font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> </span><em style="font-size: 16px;"><span style="font-style: normal; font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><a href="list/pra90_0323142014.pdf">Phys. Rev. A <strong>90, </strong>032314</a> (2014) and </span></em><span style="font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><a href="list/prl114_0804012015.pdf" style="font-size: 13px;">Phys. Rev. Lett. <strong>114, </strong>080401</a></span><span style="font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> (2015) </span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">for more details. Multiqubit symmetric states can always be written in the form</span></p>
											
											<div class="not-first-item wide center">
												<div style="" class="graphic">
													<div class="figure-content HTMLElement">
														<span>
														<div style="line-height:1.35em; font-family: Avenir Next; font-size: 13px;">
														
														<body>
														$$|\psi\rangle=\mathcal{N}\sum_{\sigma\in S_{N}}|\epsilon_{\sigma(1)}\rangle\otimes|\epsilon_{\sigma(2)}\rangle\otimes\cdots\otimes|\epsilon_{\sigma(N)}\rangle$$
														</body>
														
														</div>
														</span>
													</div>
												</div>
											</div><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">where to each single-qubit state (</span><span style="font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">for </span><span style="font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">i=1,&#8230;,N</em></span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">)</span></p>
											
											<div class="not-first-item wide center">
												<div style="" class="graphic">
													<div class="figure-content HTMLElement">
														<span>
														<div style="line-height:1.35em; font-family: Avenir Next; font-size: 13px;">
														
														<body>
														$$|\epsilon_{i}\rangle=\cos(\tfrac{\theta_i}{2})|0\rangle+\sin(\tfrac{\theta_i}{2})e^{i\varphi_i}|1\rangle$$
														</body>
														
														</div>
														</span>
													</div>
												</div>
											</div>
											<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">one can associate a point on the Bloch sphere. Therefore, multiqubit symmetric states can be described by a set of </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-size: 13px; font-style: italic; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">N</span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-size: 13px; font-style: normal; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> points on the Bloch sphere. This is the celebrated Majorana representation.</span></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">One can wonder whether the notions of anticoherent spin state to order </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">t</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> and 3-dimensional spherical </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">t</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">-design are related. Some relationships were indeed found in J. Phys. A: Math. Theor. <strong>43</strong> (2010) 255307, where it was conjectured that any spin state is anticoherent to order <em>t</em></span><span style="font-style: normal; font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> if and only if its Majorana points form a </span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">3-d spherical </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">t</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">-design. This conjecture was soon after proven false, see J. Phys. A: Math. Theor. </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><strong style="font-size: 13px;">44</strong></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> (2011) 343002. In fact, there are anticoherent states to order </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">t</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> that do not define a 3-d spherical </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">t</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">-design and, conversely, there are 3-d spherical </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">t</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">-designs that do not correspond to anticoherent states to order </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">t</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">. For example, the spin-<em>5/2</em></span><span style="font-style: normal; font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> state (expressed in the standard angular momentum </span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">basis</span><span style="font-style: normal; font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">)</span></p>
											
											<div class="not-first-item wide center">
												<div style="" class="graphic">
													<div class="figure-content HTMLElement">
														<span>
														<div style="line-height:1.35em; font-family: Avenir Next; font-size: 13px;">
														
														<body>
														$$|\psi\rangle=\sqrt{\tfrac{3}{8}}|\tfrac{5}{2},-\tfrac{5}{2}\rangle+\sqrt{\tfrac{5}{8}}|\tfrac{5}{2},\tfrac{3}{2}\rangle$$
														</body>
														
														</div>
														</span>
													</div>
												</div>
											</div><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">is anticoherent to order <em>1</em></span><span style="font-style: normal; font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> but its Majorana points do not form a spherical </span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em>1</em></span><span style="font-style: normal; font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">-design. Conversely, the </span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">spin-<em>5/2</em> state</span></p>
											
											<div class="not-first-item wide center">
												<div style="" class="graphic">
													<div class="figure-content HTMLElement">
														<span>
														<div style="line-height:1.35em; font-family: Avenir Next; font-size: 13px;">
														
														<body>
														$$|\psi\rangle=\tfrac{9}{\sqrt{206}}|\tfrac{5}{2},-\tfrac{5}{2}\rangle+5\sqrt{\tfrac{5}{206}}|\tfrac{5}{2},\tfrac{3}{2}\rangle$$
														</body>
														
														</div>
														</span>
													</div>
												</div>
											</div><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">has Majorana points forming a spherical <em>1</em>-design but is not anticoherent to order </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">1.</em></span></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">Nonetheless, numerical results seem to suggest that the pairs <em>(t,j)</em> for which a spin-<em>j</em> state anticoherent to order <em>t </em>exists coincide with those for which a <em>2j</em>-points spherical <em>t</em>-design exists in three dimensions. It has been conjectured (see the Conclusion of Phys. Scr. <strong>90</strong> (2015) 108008) that the highest possible degree of anticoherence matches the maximal degree <em>t</em> for an <em>N</em>-point spherical <em>t</em>-design. We were able to find anticoherent states for almost all <em>N</em></span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">&#8804;100 </em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; font-style: normal; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">(all </span><span style="font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em>N</em></span><span style="font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">&#8804;100</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> except</span><span style="font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular; color: rgb(0, 0, 0);"> <em>29,87 </em></span><span style="font-style: normal; font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular; color: rgb(0, 0, 0);">and</span><span style="font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular; color: rgb(0, 0, 0);"><em> 94)</em>, whose order </span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">matches the maximal degree </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">t</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> for an </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">N</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">-point spherical </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">t</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">-design</span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">.</em></span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> Despite many efforts, we were unable to find an anticoherent state to order 6 of 29 qubits, eventhough there exists a spherical 6-design made of 29 points. The state that comes closest to an anticoherent state to order 6 that we could find has a purity-based measure of anticoherence (see </span><span style="font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><a href="list/pra96_0323042017.pdf" target="_blank" style="color: rgb(148, 17, 0);">Phys. Rev. A <strong>96</strong>, 032304 (2017)</a> for more details</span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">)</span></p>
											
											<div class="not-first-item wide center">
												<div style="" class="graphic">
													<div class="figure-content HTMLElement">
														<span>
														<div style="line-height:1.35em; font-family: Avenir Next; font-size: 13px;">
														
														<body>
														$$\mathcal{A}_t(|\psi\rangle)=\frac{t+1}{t}\left(1-\mathrm{Tr}[\rho_t^2]\right)\quad\mathrm{with}\quad \rho_t=\mathrm{Tr}_{\neg t}[|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|]$$
														</body>
														
														</div>
														</span>
													</div>
												</div>
											</div><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">that is approximately equal to 1&#8722;3</span><span style="font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0);">x10<sup>-8</sup>&#8800;1</em></span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">. This raises the question of the validity of the conjecture. It might be that the maximal degree </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">t</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> for an </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="font-size: 13px;">N</em></span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">-point spherical design is only an upper bound for the maximal order of anticoherence of an <em>N/2</em>-spin state. Further work is needed to confirm or refute this conjecture.</span></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">It should be noted that as soon as we impose a minimal degeneracy </span><span style="font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0);">d</em></span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-size: 13px; font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> to one of the points defining a spherical design or a symmetric multiqubit state, the simultaneous</span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"> existence of anticoherent states and spherical designs for fixed <em>N,</em> <em>t </em></span><span style="font-style: normal; font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;">and</span><span style="font-size: 13px; color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: AvenirNext-Regular;"><em> d</em> is not observed. For example, we find that there is no spherical 2-design of 7 points with one point 2-fold degenerated while there is a spin-7/2 state anticoherent to order 2 with one Majorana point 2-fold degenerated, i.e. the state</span></p>
											
											<div class="not-first-item wide right">
												<div style="" class="graphic">
													<div class="figure-content HTMLElement">
														<span>
														<div style="line-height:1.35em; font-family: Avenir Next; font-size: 13px;">
														
														<body>
														$$|\psi\rangle=\sqrt{\tfrac{7}{10}}|\tfrac{7}{2},-\tfrac{3}{2}\rangle+\sqrt{\tfrac{3}{10}}|\tfrac{7}{2},\tfrac{7}{2}\rangle$$
														</body>
														
														</div>
														</span>
